## Summary

`eigsolve` is the second constructed-operator gate at L1 (after `ksp_solve`): the mutation-lifted eigenmode solve `result = eigsolve(E, control)` over a construction-bound `EigSolver[problem]` value, composing against `ksp_solve` for spectral-transformation modes. It has been `rough-in (test-coverage-bounded)` since cycle-009, with the qualifier framed as "algebraic-law confidence reduced pending a dedicated `test-eigensolver.cpp` OR expanded literature anchoring." **Route (b) — law-confidence re-eval — closes the gate now.** On re-reading the Palace source bodies the laws rest on, all six algebraic laws are **syntactic identities on fully-specified positive source**, not convergence-semantics conjectures: the per-pair residual `‖(K−λM)x‖₂` is *computed, stored, and exposed* in `RescaleEigenvectors`/`GetResidualNorm`/`GetError` (law 1); the eigenvector normalization `xscale = 1/‖x‖` (or `1/‖x‖_B`) is *performed* in `RescaleEigenvectors`/`GetEigenvectorNorm` (law 2); the count→status structural map reads `Solve()→int` (law 3); the shift/scaling un-transform is *performed at the accessor boundary* uniformly across all four backends (laws 4-5, confirmed by the cycle-012 lowering-verifier 4-backend audit); inner linearity is transitive from firm `apply_linop` (law 6). The convergence-test *threshold* relationship (residual < tol ⟹ reported converged) is delegated to SLEPc/ARPACK/PETSc — **exactly as `ksp_solve` delegates convergence to its underlying Krylov method, and `ksp_solve` is firm on that basis.** This is the precise `apply_nonlinear_pencil` / `chebyshev-smoother` situation: laws that are operator-algebra facts on read positive source are `firm` even when the surrounding area has no dedicated unit test. The two L1-constructive fields (`LinearSolveFailed`, `iterations`) do NOT gate the laws (they are already-resolved L1-introductions with negative-anchor treatment and L1>L0 materialization deferral; they are not law substrate). The cycle-012 dep-map authors of the two firm siblings already anticipated this: both `chebyshev-smoother` (`L1/index.md:40`) and `apply_nonlinear_pencil` (`L1/index.md:79`) explicitly note "the `eigsolve` rough-in precedent does not bind: `eigsolve`'s rough-in was driven by literature-inferred convergence semantics absent here" — this harvest finds that framing over-stated (the laws are NOT literature-inferred convergence claims; they are positive-source post-condition identities), which is exactly what unblocks the firm flip.
