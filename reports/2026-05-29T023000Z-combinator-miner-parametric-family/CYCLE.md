# CYCLE: Combinator candidate — inner-product reduce-to-scalar fold family

## Summary

Running the new **parametric/variadic-family detection mode** (cycle-018 meta-phase enactment) across the Palace `linalg/` reduction surface, the L1 BLAS-1-reduction cohort, and the partial L2 artifact, I confirm that the **`inner_product` reduce-to-scalar fold** is an independently-surfacing parametric family — NOT by same-shape instance-counting of one fixed signature, but by **three structured parameter axes folding through a single combining step** `acc + kernel(x[i], y[i])`. The cohort is the L1 leaves `dot` (Hermitian), `tdot` / `TransposeDot` (unconjugated bilinear), and the M-weighted member realized by `bilinear-form` / `matrix-weighted-norm` (Palace computes `yᴴ M x` — `Dot` conjugates its second operand), plus the `nrm2`/`Norml2` degenerate `y = x` member. The parameter axes are **conjugation convention** (conjugate-vs-not the first operand), **element-type** (real `Vector` vs complex `ComplexVector` — the complex fold is the real fold lifted componentwise over (Re, Im)), and **weight presence** (`M = I` vs pre-applied `M`). The single variadic/parametric parent is the L2 `inner_product` fold `(Tensor[N], Tensor[N]) -> Scalar ≡ foldl (+) zero (zipWith kernel x y)`, with the weighted member computed by Palace as `inner_product_M(x, M, y) = yᴴ (M·x) = yᴴ M x` (`Dot` conjugates its second operand; the L2 entry separately pins arg-1 `xᴴ` to match the L1 leaves). **This family belongs at L2** — the leaves are L1, the unifying fold is the L2 combinator that lowers to them via a conjugation/weight dispatch theme. This precisely mirrors the `linear_combination` precedent (L1 leaves → L2 fold → L2>L1 arity-dispatch theme). **Note:** cycle-018 already landed the `inner_product` L2 rough-in row by the ≥3-instance same-shape bar; this report is therefore primarily a **mode-validation** result — the parametric-family mode *would have surfaced this cohort as one candidate on its own*, validating that the new mode catches fold-families that arity-blind instance-counting misses (the BLAS-1 miss that forced a human raise). I also probed an adjacent candidate (the smoother/constructed-operator-action cohort) and report below why it is NOT a fold-family and falls outside this mode.

## Proposed combinator

- **Slug**: `inner_product` (ALREADY EXISTS as a cycle-018 L2 rough-in / cycle-019 stub — this report adds the family-mode characterization and the explicit fold-law + over-unification guard to firm the harvester's job, NOT a new row).
- **Layer**: **L2** (with rationale below).
- **Signature sketch** (best guess; harvester firms):
  ```text
  inner_product   :: (x: Tensor[N], y: Tensor[N]) -> Scalar
  inner_product_M :: (x: Tensor[N], M: LinearOperator[N, N], y: Tensor[N]) -> Scalar
  -- inner_product   ≡ foldl (+) zero (zipWith kernel x y)
  -- Palace L0 form: inner_product_M x M y ≡ inner_product (apply_linop M x) y = yᴴ (M·x)  -- weighted ≡ pre-apply M to x, then plain Dot (conjugates 2nd operand y)
  -- inner_product ≡ inner_product_M x I y                          -- plain ≡ M = I
  ```
- **Algebraic intuition**:
  - **Sesquilinearity** (complex): conjugate-linear in arg 1, linear in arg 2 (`dot`); **bilinear** (real, and `tdot`).
  - **Conjugate-symmetry**: `inner_product(x, y) = conj(inner_product(y, x))` (Hermitian member); `inner_product_tdot(x, y) = inner_product_tdot(y, x)` (bilinear member, no conj).
  - **PSD at the diagonal**: `inner_product(x, x) ≥ 0` real, `= 0 ⟺ x = 0` (exact arithmetic) — this is what `nrm2` squares-roots.
  - **Identity element of the fold**: `zero` (the additive identity of the scalar field). Empty-vector inner product = `0`.
  - **Non-associativity of the reduction tree is load-bearing** (CLAUDE.md): the fold's combining-`(+)` is FP-non-associative; the pinned Hypre+MPI tree is preserved as an explicit non-law, NOT erased.
- **Variant axes** (the parameter axes of the family):
  1. **Conjugation convention** — `kernel = conj(x[i]) * y[i]` (`dot`, Hermitian) vs `kernel = x[i] * y[i]` (`tdot`, bilinear). The ONLY per-element difference between `Dot` and `TransposeDot`.
  2. **Element-type** — real `Vector` vs complex `ComplexVector`. The complex fold is the real fold applied componentwise to (Re, Im) parts (`palace/linalg/vector.cpp:674-685`): `Re = LocalDot(xr,yr)+LocalDot(xi,yi)`, `Im = LocalDot(xi,yr)−LocalDot(xr,yi)`. Real member has no imaginary track. (Concept-page sibling of `linear_combination`'s `scalar-promotion` axis.)
  3. **Weight presence** — `M = I` (plain) vs general/ SPD `M` pre-applied to arg 1.
  4. **Diagonal degeneration** — `y = x` collapses to the norm-squared (consumed by `nrm2` / `matrix-weighted-norm`), and triggers the `&x == &y` self-dot fast path (transparent trick, `palace/linalg/vector.cpp:266,272-273`).

### The unifying fold-law (this is the evidence the cohort is ONE family, not a cluster)

All siblings are `foldl (+) zero (zipWith kernel x y)` differing ONLY in (a) the per-element `kernel`, (b) the scalar field, (c) an optional pre-`apply_linop M` on arg 1. The law that makes it a fold (not a coincidental cluster):

- **Distributivity over vector concatenation (split-additivity of the reduction):**
  `inner_product (x₁ ++ x₂) (y₁ ++ y₂) = inner_product x₁ y₁ + inner_product x₂ y₂`
  i.e. the fold is a **monoid homomorphism from (concatenated-length vectors, ++) to (Scalar, +)**. This is the inner-product analogue of `linear_combination`'s concatenation-homomorphism `lc (p ++ q) = lc p + lc q`. It holds for every member (the kernel is applied elementwise, then summed) and is exactly what licenses tiling/blocking of the reduction (the transparent HPC trick the L2 fold absorbs). The weighted member inherits it because `apply_linop` distributes over the operand when `M` is block-diagonal w.r.t. the split; in general the weighted split-law holds at the whole-vector level (`(M·x)ᴴ y` is one fold over the M-applied operand).
- **Sesquilinearity / bilinearity** (above) is the second law family that holds uniformly across members.

If a candidate did not satisfy split-additivity it would not be in this family — that is the membership test.

### Over-unification guard (REQUIRED — stated explicitly)

- **`inner_product` (reduce-to-`Scalar`) MUST NOT be merged with `linear_combination` (reduce-to-`Tensor[N]`).** They share the *operand shape* (two/many tensors of length `N`) and both are folds, but:
  - **Different result type**: `inner_product :: ... -> Scalar`; `linear_combination :: ... -> Tensor[N]`.
  - **Different combining step**: `inner_product`'s step is `acc_scalar + kernel(x[i], y[i])` (collapses the length axis); `linear_combination`'s step is `acc_vec + a·t` (preserves the length axis, accumulates over the *term* axis).
  - **Different unifying law**: `inner_product`'s homomorphism is **(length-concat, ++) → (Scalar, +)** (collapses `N`); `linear_combination`'s is **(term-list-concat, ++) → (Tensor[N], +)** (collapses the term list, keeps `N`). They fold over *different axes*.
  - This is the same boundary the cycle-017/018 `linear_combination` work drew explicitly (`book/src/L2/linear_combination.md` dep-map: "Sibling fold (do NOT merge): `dot`"). This report reaffirms it from the `inner_product` side.
- **`inner_product` MUST NOT subsume `nrm2` / `matrix-weighted-norm`.** Those are `√ ∘ inner_product` at `y = x` — *consumers*, composing a `√` post-step. The norm is not a fold member; it is a downstream composition. (The L2 `inner_product` stub already records this: "Consumer (NOT an instance): `matrix-weighted-norm`".)
- **`inner_product` MUST NOT subsume the smoother/constructed-operator-action cohort** (`JacobiSmoother`, `ChebyshevSmoother`, `DistRelaxationSmoother`, all `: public Solver<OperType>`) — see Mode validation: that cohort is a parametric family along element-type + relaxation-kernel, but it is **operator-action-shaped (`Tensor[N] -> Tensor[N]`), not a fold**, so it is out of scope for variadic-fold detection and is already partly captured by `chebyshev-smoother` + the `nested-constructed-operator-gate` concept.

## Mode validation

**Did the parametric/variadic-family detection mode perform as specified? — Yes, with two qualifications worth feeding the next meta-phase.**

1. **The mode independently surfaces the cohort as ONE candidate.** Run in parametric-family mode, the trigger "a cohort of operators whose signatures are the same up to a structured parameter axis, sharing one folding combining step" fires cleanly on `dot`/`tdot`/weighted-`Dot`: same operand shape `(Tensor[N], Tensor[N])`, same combining step `acc + kernel`, differing only along conjugation / element-type / weight. The unifying split-additivity homomorphism is stateable. This is the validation the friction-ledger entry `combinator-miner-arity-blind-parametric-family-detection` asked for: **the mode catches a fold-family that arity-blind same-shape counting would only see as 3 separate too-thin leaves** (each kernel variant, counted alone, is 1-3 sites — `tdot` is literally 0 call sites). Had the BLAS-1 case been run through *this* lens it would have surfaced `linear_combination` without a human raise; the analogous inner-product case confirms the lens generalizes to the **conjugation-convention / element-type / weight-presence** axes (not just arity).

2. **Qualification A — the candidate already existed (mode is validated, not first-to-surface).** Cycle-018's same-shape pass already cleared the ≥3-instance bar for `inner_product` (dot + tdot + bilinear-form as three same-shape `-> Scalar` reductions) and landed the rough-in. So this dispatch could not produce a *novel* row; its value is confirming the **two modes are complementary and concordant** — same-shape and parametric-family both point at the same L2 fold, and parametric-family additionally supplies the *fold-law membership test* and the *axis enumeration* that same-shape counting does not. That extra structure is what the harvester needs to firm the entry without re-deriving it. Recommend the meta-phase note that **parametric-family mode's distinctive deliverable is the fold-law + axis taxonomy, even when same-shape already surfaced the row** — i.e. the mode earns its keep as a *characterizer*, not only as a *surfacer*.

3. **Qualification B — a genuine gap: the mode has no verdict for non-fold parametric families.** My broad scan turned up a strong parametric cohort that the mode's spec does NOT cover: the **smoother / constructed-operator-action family** — `JacobiSmoother` (`palace/linalg/jacobi.hpp:19`), `ChebyshevSmoother` + `ChebyshevSmoother1stKind` (`palace/linalg/chebyshev.hpp:23,86`), `DistRelaxationSmoother` (`palace/linalg/distrelaxation.hpp:30`), all `: public Solver<OperType>`, all templated over `<Operator>` / `<ComplexOperator>` (element-type axis, e.g. `palace/linalg/jacobi.cpp:106-107`, `chebyshev.cpp:295-299`), all implementing one `Mult(x, y)` "fixed-cost relaxation action with nonzero-initial-guess flag" shape parameterized by a relaxation kernel (Jacobi diagonal-scale / Chebyshev polynomial / hybrid DistRelaxation). This is unmistakably a *parametric family along element-type + relaxation-kernel* — BUT it is **`Tensor[N] -> Tensor[N]` operator-action-shaped, not a fold (no `-> Scalar`/`-> Tensor[N]` reduction, no combining-step identity, no concatenation-homomorphism)**. The current spec's family-detection triggers and the "if you cannot state a unifying fold/parametric law it is NOT a parametric family" guard correctly *exclude* it from a fold-candidate proposal — but the spec gives **no positive channel** for "this IS a structured parametric family, just not a fold one." It is already partly captured (`book/src/L1/chebyshev-smoother.md` + the `nested-constructed-operator-gate` concept), so no row is lost here, but a future non-fold parametric cohort with no existing capture could fall through the crack. **Recommendation for the meta-phase:** extend the parametric-family mode (or add a sibling note) to recognize **constructed-operator-action families** (parametric over kernel + element-type, unified by a shared `Solver<OperType>::Mult` action contract rather than a fold-law) as a distinct, reportable family class — the unifying law there is the *action contract* (`y' = relax(op, x, y, initial_guess)` with a per-kernel `scalars`/`diagonal` generator), not a fold. Without this, the mode is fold-complete but parametric-family-incomplete.

**Net:** mode performed to spec on the fold case (validated), and its boundary against `linear_combination` and against non-fold cohorts held correctly. The one actionable gap is the absent positive channel for non-fold parametric families (Qualification B).

## Open questions / caveats

- **Conjugation-convention contradiction between Palace source and the L1 `dot.md` entry.** Palace documents `Dot(comm, x, y) = yᴴ x` (the *second* argument conjugated — `vector.hpp:247`, and `ComplexVector::Dot(y)` returns `yᴴ·(*this)` so receiver is linear, *argument* conjugated). But `book/src/L1/dot.md:34,43` states the convention as "conjugate-linear in the **first** argument" / "the L1 signature names the conjugated argument first" — i.e. the L1 form *deliberately re-orders* so arg-1 is the conjugated one. This is an intentional L1 mutation-rotation choice (erasing the method receiver/argument asymmetry), not an error — BUT the harvester firming `inner_product` MUST pin ONE convention and state the re-order explicitly, because the M-weighted member `Dot(comm, x, A, y) = yᴴ (Ax) = yᴴ A x` conjugates the *second* operand `y` (Palace's `Dot` convention; `Ax` is pre-applied to the first operand and is NOT itself conjugated), and `linear_combination`'s sibling-boundary prose assumes a fixed convention. The L2 stub (`book/src/L2/inner_product.md:7`) already flags "exact conjugation/arg-order convention to be pinned" — this report raises it to a hard must-resolve-before-firm. Tracked by OQ `inner-product-harvester-formalization-and-conjugation-pinning`.
- **`tdot` / `TransposeDot` has zero call sites in Palace.** It is part of `ComplexVector`'s API surface (declared + defined) but unexercised in the tree. As a *family member* its evidentiary weight is type-API-only, not behavioral. Two readings: (a) keep it as a family member (it is a real, defined kernel and a legitimate axis value — the unconjugated bilinear form), with a "no Palace call site" caveat like `bilinear-form`'s narrow-coverage note; or (b) demote it to a documented-but-not-instantiated axis value. Recommend (a) for completeness of the conjugation axis, with the caveat recorded. Does the harvester's `inner_product` firm-up cite `tdot` as firm (matching `book/src/L1/dot.md` which treats it as a co-defined operator) or rough-in? Flagging for the harvester.
- **Smoother-family positive channel (see Mode validation Qualification B)** — should the parametric-family mode spec gain a non-fold "constructed-operator-action family" class? Filing as the actionable mode-gap for the next meta-phase; does not block this dispatch.
- **Weighted split-additivity is only whole-vector general.** The split-additivity homomorphism `inner_product(x₁++x₂)(y₁++y₂) = …` holds elementwise for the plain/conjugate members, but for `inner_product_M` the per-block split requires `M` block-diagonal w.r.t. the split. The whole-vector form always holds (one fold over the M-applied operand). The harvester should state the law at whole-vector granularity to avoid over-claiming a per-block tiling law for the weighted member.
