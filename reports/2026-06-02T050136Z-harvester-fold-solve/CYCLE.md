# fold_solve

The L4 **state-threaded fold outer-driver combinator**: thread a persistent field-state carry through a schedule of steps, advancing it one opaque per-step operator (`time_step_op`) at a time, where **each step's input is the prior step's output**, and return the final state. Where [`solve_family`](./solve_family.md) is the **independent map** over an RHS family (no carry between elements; the trajectory IS the collected family), `fold_solve` is its **sequential fold sibling** — the previous step's output is the next step's input, so the steps do NOT commute and cannot be collected independently. Both are children of the strawman §3.7 [`iterate-while`](./iterate-while.md) family (RATIFIED batch-17 meta decision 2): a **map is the degenerate fold whose step ignores the accumulator**; `fold_solve` is the **non-degenerate** member that threads the accumulator. It reuses the firm iterate-while vocabulary rather than introducing a new iteration primitive — the same route [`chebyshev`](./chebyshev.md) and [`solve_family`](./solve_family.md) took.

## Context

L4's job is to write algorithms in a graph-evaluation calculus that makes lifetimes, dispatch sites, and effect placement structural (`L4/index.md:7-13`). `fold_solve` names the coordination shape one shell *above* a per-step solve, the fold-sibling of [`solve_family`](./solve_family.md)'s independent map:

- [`solve_family`](./solve_family.md) names the **independent-map shell** — a fixed `op`, a family `[rhs_i]` → the solution family `[x_i]`, *no carry between elements* (each solve independent; the family commutes).
- `fold_solve` (this entry) names the **sequential-fold shell** — a captured `op`, a seed state `s0`, a schedule → the final state, *carry threaded forward* (each step's input is the prior step's output; the steps do NOT commute).

The architectural altitude is the same as [`solve_family`](./solve_family.md)'s and `solve_loop`'s — all are value-threaded loop combinators in the strawman's §3.7 family (`book/src/design/l4_calculus.md:150-184`). The combinator differs only by the **map/fold axis**: [`solve_family`](./solve_family.md) is the **pure-map degenerate** (each element independent, no carry), `fold_solve` is the **non-degenerate fold** (the carry is threaded). This is exactly the strawman §3.7 reading: `iterate_while` threads a `state` carry forward; a map is the special case where the step's output does not depend on the prior carry, a fold the general case where it does. The §3.7 `iterate_while` family **IS** the shared parent of both — no third parent abstraction is warranted (RATIFIED batch-17 meta decision 2; `L4/index.md:62`).

The combinator is defined **in L4 vocabulary** (high→low discipline, CLAUDE.md §Methodology invariants): its semantics, signature, and laws are stated in terms of the [`iterate-while`](./iterate-while.md) family, the [`state-stratification`](../concepts/state-stratification.md) operator/carry strata, and the [`sequential-obstruction`](../concepts/sequential-obstruction.md) of the carry threading — NOT in terms of L3 value-threading or the L0 C++ time-loop. The L4>L3 dissolution (the `foldl` collapsing to an explicit `for`-loop threading the field-state in place, the per-step body a role-naming wrapper over the opaque MFEM `ODESolver` step) is a separate cycle-058 L4>L3 theme (`fold-solve-time-step-dissolution`, authored by D2 this same cycle), narrated forward from L4 to L3; it is **not** authored here.

`fold_solve` at L4 is a **methodology-level combinator** distilled from two Palace driver-source state-threaded sweeps (transient time-march + driven-PROM greedy basis construction), not a Palace-source artefact per se — there is no single L0 range that "is" the L4 `fold_solve`. The Palace evidence is the two sweeps in §Specializations; L4 names the fold combinator and the carry-threading those two sweeps share.

## Signature

The combinator captures the operator once, seeds the carry once, and threads the carry through the schedule by `foldl`. The **default surface** is the fixed-list schedule (transient witness); the state-generated-schedule generalization is the `schedule-source` variant axis (§Variant axes) rendered through the §3.7 carry form.

    -- entry point (default surface): fixed-list state-threaded fold
    -- capture op once, seed s0 once, thread the carry through a precomputed schedule
    fold_solve :: OpParams -> TimeState -> [Time] -> TimeState
    fold_solve op s0 schedule = foldl (\s t -> time_step_op op s t) s0 schedule

    -- the opaque per-step operator the combinator quantifies over:
    -- advances the field-state one step; bottoms out in a library integrator
    time_step_op :: OpParams -> TimeState -> Time -> TimeState

    -- equivalently, as the NON-degenerate member of the strawman §3.7 iterate_while family
    -- (carry = field-state + schedule-state; the step reads the prior carry to advance it):
    fold_solve op s0 schedule =
      (iterate_while
         { field: s0, remaining: schedule }      -- carry: persistent field-state + schedule-state
         (\c -> not (null c.remaining))            -- continue while schedule remains
         (\c -> let t  = head c.remaining
                    s' = time_step_op op c.field t -- prior FIELD is read: this is the fold, not a map
                in { state: { field: s', remaining: tail c.remaining } }))
        .final_state.field                         -- == foldl (\s t -> time_step_op op s t) s0 schedule

Shape contract (bunsen-style; named records and axes; the operator/carry strata per [`state-stratification`](../concepts/state-stratification.md)):

- `OpParams` — operator-internal configuration, **captured once at construction; `readonly`** per [`state-stratification`](../concepts/state-stratification.md). This is the L4 typing of the `TimeOperator time_op(iodata, space_op, dJdt_coef)` built *once* outside the loop (transient `transientsolver.cpp:33`), whose ODE-integrator `op` is constructed once (`timeoperator.cpp:312`). `op` is the *shared* operator stratum threaded *unchanged* into every `time_step_op` call.
- `TimeState` — the persistent field-state carry the fold threads forward. For transient it is the coupled `(E, B)` field bundle (the `sol` vector `timeoperator.cpp:410` advances in place); each step reads the *prior* `TimeState` and produces the next. **This is what makes the combinator a fold and not a map**: `time_step_op op s_k t_{k+1}` reads `s_k` (the prior step's output), so the steps are strictly sequential and do not commute.
- `[Time]` (default surface) — the **fixed** schedule the fold ranges over: a precomputed uniform list of `n_step` timesteps (transient `n_step = config::GetNumSteps(...)` `transientsolver.cpp:36`, uniform `delta_t` `transientsolver.cpp:35`). The schedule-source is the load-bearing variant axis: for the state-generated form (SweepAdaptive) the schedule is *generated* from the carry, not consumed from a fixed list (§Variant axes, §Specializations).
- result `TimeState` — the final field-state after the whole schedule is threaded. Only `.final_state` is observed (per §3.8 demand-pruning the trajectory of intermediate states is pruned unless a per-step consumer reads it — and transient DOES consume each step's `(E, B)` for postprocessing `transientsolver.cpp:98-99`, so the trajectory materializes in practice).
- `FoldCarry = { field: TimeState, remaining: [Time] }` — the `iterate_while` carry in the §3.7-form rendering; transient (the `foldl` form `foldl (time_step_op op) s0 schedule` does not name it). For the state-generated form the carry additionally holds the schedule-generating state (the growing reduced basis + error history — §Specializations).

The shape contract makes two things structural at the fold level that are merely conventional in the Palace C++ sweep:

1. **The operator is captured once, outside the fold.** `op : OpParams` appears once in the signature, bound before the fold; every per-step `time_step_op` reads the *same* `op`. This is the L4 typing of `TimeOperator` built once outside the time loop (transient `transientsolver.cpp:33` / `timeoperator.cpp:312`, outside the `:77` loop).
2. **The carry is threaded; the steps are sequential.** `TimeState` is threaded forward by the fold; each `time_step_op` reads the prior carry. This forbids reordering at the type level — the property [`solve_family`](./solve_family.md)'s map does **not** have (its elements are independent and commute). The map/fold distinction is exactly the carry-dependence: a map's step ignores the accumulator, a fold's reads it.

## Semantics

`fold_solve op s0 schedule` is the complete state-threaded march expressed as a `foldl` of the opaque per-step operator `time_step_op` over the schedule, seeded by `s0`, with a single shared operator capture. It has two equivalent presentations (the §Signature renders both):

1. **Direct fold form** — `fold_solve op s0 schedule = foldl (\s t -> time_step_op op s t) s0 schedule`. The combinator *is* a left fold: the seed `s0` is threaded through the schedule, each step advancing the field-state by one opaque `time_step_op`. This is the form the combinator is named for, and the form that makes the algebraic laws (§Algebraic laws) immediate.

2. **Non-degenerate member of the §3.7 `iterate_while` family** — the family rendering (`book/src/design/l4_calculus.md:150-184`), where the carry is `{ field, remaining }`, the predicate is `not (null c.remaining)`, the step pops one `Time`, reads the prior `field`, and runs one `time_step_op`. By §3.7 the `.final_state.field` is exactly `foldl (time_step_op op) s0 schedule`. This presentation reuses the firm [`iterate-while`](./iterate-while.md) family rather than introducing a new iteration vocabulary (the [`chebyshev`](./chebyshev.md) / [`solve_family`](./solve_family.md) route, `L4/index.md:37`); the choice between the two presentations is a presentation rotation, not a semantic distinction.

The combinator's **structural contrast with [`solve_family`](./solve_family.md)** — the reason it is a distinct entry rather than a reuse of the map — is the **carry-dependence of the step**. [`solve_family`](./solve_family.md)'s step `ksp_solve op inp` ignores any accumulator (each element independent → the map's concatenation-homomorphism, element-independence, embarrassing-parallelism laws). `fold_solve`'s step `time_step_op op s t` reads `s` (the prior carry) → the steps are sequential, do NOT commute, and do NOT split into independent partitions. This is the §3.7 reading of the map/fold axis: the map is the degenerate fold whose step is constant in the accumulator; `fold_solve` is the non-degenerate fold whose step threads it. The two are siblings under the one §3.7 parent, not instances of one combinator.

Per [`state-stratification`](../concepts/state-stratification.md), `op` is the shared `readonly` operator stratum captured once; `TimeState` is the threaded persistent carry stratum. The fold does not introduce a new monadic effect — the per-step `time_step_op` discharges its own step effect (advancing the field-state) and the fold threads the result. The combinator is therefore a **pure function** `(op, s0, schedule) -> TimeState` (modulo whatever non-determinism the opaque per-step operator inherits; the fold introduces none).

### The opaque per-step body

`time_step_op` bottoms out in an **opaque library step** the L4 entry only quantifies over — it does not render the per-step body. For transient the step is the MFEM `ODESolver::Step` call (`timeoperator.cpp:410` `ode->Step(sol, t, dt)`, dispatched from `transientsolver.cpp:93` `time_op.Step(t, delta_t)`); the integrator (`GeneralizedAlphaSolver` / `SDIRK23Solver` / `ARKStepSolver`) is selected at construction (`timeoperator.cpp:312`+) and is library-owned. At L4 the entry names the per-step operator by role (`time_step_op`) and quantifies over it; **the opacity is a lowering-layer concern** — the L4>L3 theme `fold-solve-time-step-dissolution` (cycle-058 D2) records the per-step body as `obstruction (opaque-library-ownership)` (the `eigsolve` opaque-library shape, `L4/eigsolve.md`), NOT a Palace-authored loop. At L4 the entry just folds over the opaque operator.

### Demand-pruning interaction

Under the §3.8 pruning rule (`book/src/design/l4_calculus.md:186-228`), the **threaded carry is never pruned** (every step's `field` is read by the next step's `time_step_op` — the fold's defining data-dependence), but the per-step *trajectory* of intermediate states is pruned when no consumer reads it. In Palace's transient sweep each step's `(E, B)` IS consumed immediately (postprocessing per step: `transientsolver.cpp:98` `time_op.GetE()` / `:99` `time_op.GetB()`), so the trajectory materializes in practice; but the combinator's typing makes the demand-driven materialization of intermediate states structural (only the `final_state` survives when intermediate states are unobserved — the §3.7 trajectory-pruning the fold inherits).

## Algebraic laws

`fold_solve` is a **`foldl` combinator**, so its laws are the laws of a left fold over an opaque step specialised to the captured-operator march. Absences are catalogued explicitly to prevent decoration drift. Every law is a **syntactic identity on the read fold** — read directly off the two positive driver loops (the firm-on-positive-structure basis for the `firm` status).

1. **Fold-threading associativity / schedule-split** (the load-bearing law). `fold_solve op s0 (a ++ b) = fold_solve op (fold_solve op s0 a) b`. The fold of a concatenated schedule is the fold of the second segment seeded by the fold of the first — the standard `foldl (a ++ b) = foldl b . foldl a` law, specialised to the captured operator. This is the algebraic statement of the carry-threading: a march can be **checkpointed and resumed** (split the schedule, fold the prefix, fold the suffix from the prefix's final state). It is the **fold analog of [`solve_family`](./solve_family.md)'s concatenation-homomorphism** — but note the difference: `solve_family` *distributes* over `++` (`f (a++b) = f a ++ f b`, independent), whereas `fold_solve` *threads* through `++` (`f s0 (a++b) = f (f s0 a) b`, sequential). The map splits into independent halves; the fold splits into a checkpoint-and-resume.

2. **Operator-capture-once / construction-hoist**. `fold_solve op s0 schedule = foldl (time_step_op op) s0 schedule`, and the `op`-dependent integrator construction is **invariant across the fold**, so it hoists out (computed once, before the schedule is threaded). This is the L4 typing of `TimeOperator` / the ODE integrator built once outside the time loop (transient `transientsolver.cpp:33` / `timeoperator.cpp:312`, outside the `:77` loop). Same hoist as [`solve_family`](./solve_family.md) law 2 — the operator capture is shared by the whole §3.7 family.

3. **Seed left-identity on the empty schedule** (`fold_solve op s0 [] = s0`). The empty schedule threads the seed unchanged — the `foldl ... s0 [] = s0` identity. A degenerate (not algebraic) identity; Palace's transient march always has `n_step > 0` (`transientsolver.cpp:36`), so the empty case is a calculus-level total-definition convenience, not a witnessed Palace path. (Contrast [`solve_family`](./solve_family.md) law 4 `f op [] = []`: the empty map yields the empty family; the empty fold yields the seed.)

Laws that explicitly **do not** hold:

- **Element-independence / commutativity / distribution over `++`** (the load-bearing *non*-law — the map/fold distinction). `fold_solve op s0 (a ++ b) ≠ fold_solve op s0 a ‹combined-with› fold_solve op s0 b` for any independent combination — because each step's input is the prior step's output. The schedule does **NOT** commute (`fold_solve op s0 (permute schedule) ≠ permute (...)`): reordering the timesteps changes the field-state trajectory. This is precisely what distinguishes `fold_solve` from [`solve_family`](./solve_family.md) (whose law 1/law 3 ARE distribution + commutativity). A fold is not embarrassingly parallel; the carry-threading is a [`sequential-obstruction`](../concepts/sequential-obstruction.md).
- **Step fusion into a single closed-form whole-march op.** The per-step `time_step_op` invocations do not fuse into one closed-form whole-schedule operator — each is an opaque library integrator step with its own internal state advance. The fold threads independent opaque steps; it does not collapse them. (The schedule-split law 1 is *checkpoint-resume*, not *fusion*.)
- **Linearity of the final state in the seed or schedule.** `time_step_op` is in general a nonlinear/implicit integrator step (the transient solver is `IMPLICIT`, `timeoperator.cpp:310`-region); the final state is not linear in `s0` nor in the schedule. No linearity law is claimed.
- **Schedule-source uniformity across the variant axis.** The fixed-list laws (1–3) hold for the **fixed-schedule** form (transient). The **state-generated** form (SweepAdaptive) generates its schedule + loop bound from the carry (the next input `omega_star = FindMaxError(state)` `drivensolver.cpp:389`, the bound `memory < convergence_memory` `drivensolver.cpp:384,398`); the schedule-split law 1 still holds *for a realized schedule* but the schedule is no longer a free `++`-decomposable input. The laws here are scoped to the fold spine both forms share; the schedule-source generalization is the `schedule-source` variant axis (§Variant axes) + OQ `fold-solve-greedy-schedule-source-generalization`.

## Specializations

Per replace-and-propagate (CLAUDE.md §Methodology invariants vocabulary-shift redirect), `fold_solve` is the **entry**; the two Palace state-threaded sweeps are **specialization notes re-expressing THROUGH it**, not separate rectangular leaf chapters. Both instantiate the state-threaded fold spine (carry threaded forward, prior output = next input, opaque per-step body); they differ on the **schedule-source** variant axis (the load-bearing difference).

- **Transient fixed-schedule time-march** (`palace/drivers/transientsolver.cpp`, `TransientSolver::Solve`). The **default surface** (the `[Time]`-list fold). `op = TimeOperator` built once (`:33`) with its ODE integrator constructed once (`timeoperator.cpp:312`) — outside the loop. The seed `s0` is the initial field-state set by `time_op.Init()` (`:89`). The schedule is a **fixed uniform** list: `n_step = config::GetNumSteps(0.0, max_t, delta_t)` (`:36`) timesteps of uniform `delta_t` (`:35`). The fold body is `time_op.Step(t, delta_t)` (`:93`) → `ode->Step(sol, t, dt)` (`timeoperator.cpp:410`), which advances the persistent `sol` field-state in place — **the prior step's `sol` is the next step's input**, the genuine `foldl`. The loop is the `for (int step = 0; step < n_step; step++)` (`:77`). Per-step `(E, B)` consumed for postprocessing (`:98`/`:99`). Element-type: real. This witness gives the fixed-list signature surface directly.

- **Driven-PROM greedy basis construction (SweepAdaptive)** (`palace/drivers/drivensolver.cpp`, `DrivenSolver::SweepAdaptive`, `:231`; dispatched `adaptive ? SweepAdaptive : SweepUniform` `:73`). The **state-generated-schedule** form — strictly richer than the fixed-list fold: the schedule (which frequencies to sample) is **generated from the carry**, not consumed from a fixed list. The carry is the growing reduced basis + error history (the `RomOperator prom_op` + `max_errors` accumulator). The greedy loop `for (std::size_t it0 = it; it < max_size_per_excitation && memory < convergence_memory; it++)` (`:384`) has a **state-derived loop bound** (`memory` is updated from per-step error `:398`; PROM greedy params `offline_tol` `:241`, `convergence_memory` `:242`, `max_size_per_excitation` `:243`, `prom_indices` `:244`) AND a **state-derived per-step input** (`omega_star = prom_op.FindMaxError(excitation_idx)[0]` `:389` — the next sample frequency is the argmax of the *current* PROM error). Each step reads the prior carry (basis + errors) and produces the next (one sample added). Element-type: complex.

Both sweeps share the **state-threaded fold spine** (carry threaded forward, prior output = next input, opaque per-step body). The difference is the **`schedule-source` axis**: transient consumes a fixed `[Time]`; SweepAdaptive generates `[omega_star_k]` from accumulated state with a state-derived termination. The fixed-list form is the special case of the §3.7 carry form where the carry's next-input is `head remaining` and the predicate is `not (null remaining)`; the greedy form is the general case where next-input and predicate are both state-derived. **Classification decision (load-bearing): ONE combinator** — the §3.7 carry form covers both; the schedule-source is the recorded variant axis, not a distinct entry.
