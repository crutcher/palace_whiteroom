# CYCLE: L4 + L4>L3 coverage survey — the L4-cap / L4>L3-theme denominator

## Summary

This is a **coverage survey + fan-out ranking**, not a single-pattern proposal. I enumerated the 18 firm/partial L3 operators on disk, crossed them against the 4 firm L4 caps and 3 firm L4>L3 themes, and judged for each gap whether an L4 cap (or L4>L3 theme) is *warranted at all*. The dominant finding: **the L4 layer is mostly intentionally complete, not under-covered.** Of the 18 L3 operators, **13 carry an explicit on-disk `CONFIRMED-NOT-NEEDED` L4 verdict** (the cycle-010 cross-layer-cross-cutter leaf-cohort audit, recorded in each L3 entry's dep-map and §"Why no L4 entry"). The L4 design axiom "vocabulary, not architecture" (`book/src/L4/index.md:7`) plus the cycle-010 leaf-primitive rule (leaf / fused-composite / constructed-operator-gate operators carry no monadic effect, no state-stratification typing, no driver structure, therefore no L4 row) make almost the entire BLAS-1 + elementwise + constructed-gate cohort **no-L4-by-design**, exactly parallel to `apply_linop` being no-L2-by-design.

The **genuinely warranted L4-layer work is narrow and bounded**: (1) the two **standalone L4>L3 dissolution themes** for `iterate-while` / `iterate-while-with-prev` (the rotation currently lives only as a sub-component of `krylov-step-typed-wrapper-dissolution` with no dedicated standalone theme, and the rendered (pruned) form there drops the `trajectory` accumulator — by §3.8 demand-pruning (the L3-side image of Law 1, `krylov-step-typed-wrapper-dissolution.md:188-198`) when the consumer reads only `final_state`; the trajectory-keeping unpruned form is also presented (`:164-171`) but is left un-anchored as a dedicated chapter — a real coverage gap flagged in both L4 entries); and (2) a future **L4 `eigsolve` / `solve-monad` outer-driver cap** (currently `ksp_solve` and `eigsolve` lift from L2 only; no `L4/ksp_solve.md` or `L4/eigsolve.md` exists, and the `solve-monad` `restart_cycle` / `solve_loop` outer drivers are referenced from concept pages but never anchored as L4 rows). Pick (1) is the highest-fan-out, lowest-cost, abstractor-shaped work for cycle-047; pick (2) is higher-fan-out but a larger, harvester-shaped multi-cycle build that depends on the `solve-monad` concept surface firming.

**`iterate-while` + `iterate-while-with-prev` (the two combinator rows) — WARRANTED, top rank.** Both have firm L4 caps. The criterion for a *theme* is "firm cap whose dissolution is not already covered." Coverage is **present-but-incomplete**: the rotation lives only inside `krylov-step-typed-wrapper-dissolution.md:156-198`, which presents BOTH an unpruned form that **keeps the trajectory** (`:164-171`, returning `reverse traj`) AND a pruned form that **drops the `trajectory` accumulator** (`:176-184`) — and the pruned form drops it *by* the §3.8 demand-pruning rule (the L3-side image of Law 1, `:188-198`) when the consumer reads only `final_state`, not in contradiction of the Law. The gap is that there is **no standalone L4>L3 theme** and the trajectory-keeping unpruned form is left un-anchored as a dedicated chapter; both entries explicitly defer the standalone theme and point at this shared sub-component. The OQ `iterate-while-l3-rendering-trajectory-accumulation-gap` is marked "answered cycle-008 — verdict-(c) collapse-rule + Condition 5 applied" in the closed index (`open-questions.md:200`), but the *answer was a collapse rule, not an authored standalone theme* — the L4 entries still point at the defective sub-component. **This is the cleanest, highest-leverage L4-layer gap**: two firm caps, a stated fidelity defect, a bounded abstractor deliverable.
