## Context

Six clean passes — first batch in the run with zero revises or downgrades. But the methodology surface signal hides a real problem: **all six cycles were retroactive_claims**. The loop landed zero new layer prose in the window. cg and gmres got their L2/L3/L4 stacks re-emitted clean (and re-audited at per-building-block granularity), but no slice advanced its frontier.

This is the predictable consequence of the meta-16 per-building-block granularity rule combined with five slices saturated at L4: backfill cycles are productive AND low-friction, so they out-compete forward dispatch in the Planner's implicit calculus. The meta-17 retroactive-budget rule was guidance-shaped; the loop ignored it.

This restructures the forward-frontier criterion from "any forward edge" to a positive selector for the highest-impact-per-cycle dispatch.
