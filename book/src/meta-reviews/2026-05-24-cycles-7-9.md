### Bonus user-introduced concept

**Constructed operators** — a peer concept to `rotation.md` and `variant-absorption.md`, introduced by the user during enactment review. Motivated by exactly the cycle-7/cycle-9 friction (deep-plumbing of `side` + `M` through every L1 step). Standard pattern from evaluation / immutable-graph traditions (Haskell, MLIR, JAX, burn): construct an operator with internal immutable state, call `apply` as a pure function. Resolves all three variant-absorption levels (a)+(b)+(c) when the variant influences operator behavior but not algorithm shape — `op = construct_krylov_operator(A, M, side)`; per-step procedure is just `op.apply(v)`.

This concept is genuinely orthogonal to (and complements) variant absorption. Variant absorption asks "did the variant axes unify?"; constructed operators provide one canonical mechanism for the unification when the variant is a configuration to be set-once-and-applied-many-times.
