# fe-assemble-fold-dissolution

The L4>L3 lowering theme for the [`fe_assemble`](../L4/fe_assemble.md) **assemble-fold combinator** — the L4 concatenation-homomorphism `fe_assemble space terms = foldr (\t acc -> assemble_term space t + acc) zero terms = Σ_{t ∈ terms} assemble_term space t` that captures the finite-element space once, folds the opaque per-term assembly leaf `assemble_term` over an **immutable, independent** weak-form term list, and reduces the per-term `LinOp[(N: ...), $N]` contributions by operator-`+` into one global operator. The theme dissolves the L4 `foldr` combinator (the once-captured `readonly` `space` stratum, the **homomorphic** map-then-reduce over the independent term family, the order-independent operator sum) into the L3 **explicit imperative integrator-fold**: a `for` over the integrator lists, one per-term sub-operator accumulated per iteration into a composite operator by `AddSubOperator`, the per-term leaf bottoming out in the **opaque libCEED element-local quadrature kernel** (`obstruction (opaque-library-ownership)` sub-leaf). It is the **homomorphic sibling** of the [`solve-family-map-dissolution`](./solve-family-map-dissolution.md) MAP-shell dissolution: both dissolve an independent family-loop with NO carry and NO `sequential-obstruction`; this theme's family is the weak-form *term* list (reduced by operator-`+`) rather than the RHS family (collected into a `std::vector`). It is **explicitly NOT** the carry-threaded [`fold-solve-time-step-dissolution`](./fold-solve-time-step-dissolution.md) fold (the map-not-fold guard, §"The map-not-fold distinction").

## Slug

`fe-assemble-fold-dissolution`

## Context

The L4 **assemble-fold combinator** [`fe_assemble`](../L4/fe_assemble.md) is the genuinely-new FE calculus vocabulary that opens the assemble-half of the deliverable's L4 surface (CLAUDE.md §directive-1; the `K`-source for every solver pipeline). The combinator's own §"Lowers to" names the dissolution to L3 and records the rotation *direction* in-line per the high→low discipline. This chapter is that theme, narrated forward from L4 to L3.

`fe_assemble` and [`solve_family`](../L4/solve_family.md) are **both homomorphic family-loops** — independent elements reduced/collected with no cross-element carry — and so their two L4>L3 dissolutions are siblings. This theme is **structurally parallel** to [`solve-family-map-dissolution`](./solve-family-map-dissolution.md). The load-bearing axis that distinguishes them is the *reduction shape* and the *leaf*:

- [`solve-family-map-dissolution`](./solve-family-map-dissolution.md) (firm) — the L4 `map` over an independent RHS family → an L3 positional accumulating `for` that **collects** each member into its own `std::vector<Vector>` slot. The per-member leaf (`ksp.Mult`) **DOES lower** (delegated to [`ksp-solve-driver-dissolution`](./ksp-solve-driver-dissolution.md)). The family loop carries **NO `sequential-obstruction`** (independent members).
- `fe-assemble-fold-dissolution` (this theme) — the L4 concatenation-homomorphism `foldr` over an independent **term** family → an L3 explicit integrator-`for` that **reduces** each per-term sub-operator into a composite by `AddSubOperator`. The per-term leaf (the libCEED element-local quadrature kernel) **does NOT lower** — it is the `obstruction (opaque-library-ownership)` sub-leaf. The term-list loop carries **NO `sequential-obstruction`** (term-position commutativity; the operator sum is order-independent).

Both are the **homomorphic** branch of the family-loop axis (independent elements, no carry); the contrast with the carry-threaded [`fold-solve-time-step-dissolution`](./fold-solve-time-step-dissolution.md) (where each step reads the prior carry — a genuine `sequential-obstruction` + an in-place-threaded state) is the **map-not-fold guard** this theme honors (§"The map-not-fold distinction").

The rotation direction is **L4 → L3**, narrated forward per the high→low discipline (CLAUDE.md §Methodology invariants "Layers are defined high→low"). Notes about the reverse lift (how the L3 imperative integrator-fold lifts back into the `foldr` combinator, what evidence licenses recovering the homomorphism from the imperative `AddSubOperator` accumulation) live in the cap's §"L4 vs L3 distinction" and in this report's working notes, not in this formal chapter.

This is a **genuine vocabulary translation, not an identity-in-named-terms rename** (CLAUDE.md §Methodology invariants vocabulary-shift redirect; a degenerate identity-in-named-terms lowering is a §1d smell). The L4 vocabulary — `foldr` producing a sum, the `readonly` once-captured `space` stratum, the **opaque quantified-over per-term leaf** `assemble_term`, the homomorphic list-fold whose concatenation-homomorphism / commutativity laws are about the LIST and operator-`+`, the single logical term list — is a *different semantic organization* from the L3 vocabulary — an explicit positional `for` over **two** integrator containers (`domain_integs` / `boundary_integs`), a per-iteration `CeedOperator sub_op` built by a pure-virtual leaf dispatch, a composite operator **mutated** by `AddSubOperator`, a `Finalize()` step, and the PA-vs-FA representation dispatch. The reorganization — a single combinator naming the whole assembly as a homomorphic sum, dissolving into an imperative build-up-then-accumulate object protocol over a Palace-owned orchestration shell with a libCEED-owned leaf — is the substance of the theme. The pieces that change across the vocabularies are catalogued in §"L3 form (RHS)".

## L4 form (LHS)

The L4 [`fe_assemble`](../L4/fe_assemble.md) combinator — the firm D1 assemble-fold shape (the firm entry §Signature). The entry point, transcribed from the firm cap:

```text
-- entry point: capture the space once, fold the opaque per-term assembly leaf over the
-- immutable term list, reduce by operator-+ into one global operator (a list-homomorphism)
fe_assemble :: FiniteElementSpace[N] -> [WeakFormTerm] -> LinOp[(N: ...), $N]
fe_assemble space terms = foldr (\t acc -> assemble_term space t + acc) zero terms
                        = Σ_{t ∈ terms} assemble_term space t

-- the opaque per-term assembly leaf the combinator quantifies over (one weak-form term to its
-- global-dof contribution; bottoms out in a libCEED element-local quadrature kernel the L4
-- entry does NOT render):
assemble_term :: FiniteElementSpace[N] -> WeakFormTerm -> LinOp[(N: ...), $N]

-- equivalently, the HOMOMORPHIC member of the strawman §3.7 iterate_while family
-- (carry = remaining term list + running operator sum; the step does NOT read the running
-- sum to compute its own contribution — the contributions are independent, hence a map-reduce
-- NOT a carry-threaded fold):
fe_assemble space terms =
  (iterate_while
     { remaining: terms, acc: zero }          -- carry: remaining terms + running operator sum
     (\c -> not (null c.remaining))            -- continue while terms remain
     (\c -> let t   = head c.remaining
                sub = assemble_term space t    -- the term's contribution is INDEPENDENT of acc
            in { state: { remaining: tail c.remaining
                        , acc: c.acc + sub } }))   -- reduce by operator-+ (commutes/associates)
    .final_state.acc                           -- == Σ_{t ∈ terms} assemble_term space t
```

The assemble-fold-shell machinery this theme dissolves is **three** pieces:

1. **The once-captured `readonly` finite-element-space stratum.** `space : FiniteElementSpace[N]` is bound *once, outside the `foldr`*, per [`state-stratification`](../concepts/state-stratification.md); it is the shared `readonly` stratum threaded *unchanged* into every per-term `assemble_term`. The `space`-dependent assembly context (the trial/test restriction + basis the leaf needs) is **invariant across the fold and hoists out of it** — the same operator/context-capture-once law the map sibling has on its `op`.

2. **The homomorphic map-then-reduce over the independent term family.** `foldr (\t acc -> assemble_term space t + acc) zero terms` applies the per-element leaf `assemble_term space` independently to each term and reduces the results by operator-`+`. **This is what makes the combinator a homomorphic family-loop and not a carry-threaded fold**: the step `assemble_term space t` computes the term's contribution *without reading the running accumulator `acc`* — `acc` is only the reduction target, not an input to the next contribution. The contributions are independent; the fold is a map-then-`Σ`.

3. **The opaque per-term assembly leaf + the single logical term list.** `assemble_term` is the opaque per-term leaf the combinator only **quantifies over** — at L4 the entry names it by role and does not render its body (the libCEED element-local quadrature kernel). `terms : [WeakFormTerm]` is the single logical term list (the L1 cap's note: `terms = domain_integs ++ boundary_integs`, the two L0 sub-lists are one L1 fold via the concatenation homomorphism).

The load-bearing L4 properties this lowering must transport are the cap's homomorphism identities (the firm L1 cap §"Algebraic laws", carried up to the L4 cap): **Law 1 (empty-term identity)** — `fe_assemble space [] = zero`, the fold's identity element; **Law 2 (concatenation homomorphism / term-list additivity)** — `fe_assemble space (a ++ b) = fe_assemble space a + fe_assemble space b`, the list-homomorphism that makes the domain/boundary two-list L0 structure a single L1/L4 fold (this is the **homomorphism** that names the family-loop as the map branch); **Law 3 (single-term reduction)** — `fe_assemble space [t] = assemble_term space t`; **Law 4 (term-position commutativity)** — `fe_assemble space terms` is invariant under permutation of `terms` (operator-`+` commutes/associates). And the load-bearing **non-law** (the map-not-fold distinction): **NO carry-dependence** — no term's contribution reads the running sum, so the term loop does NOT carry a `sequential-obstruction` (the §"What does NOT change" verdict — the contrast with `fold_solve`).

## L3 form (RHS)

The L4>L3 dissolution produces the L3 **explicit imperative integrator-fold** — the Palace C++ `BilinearForm::PartialAssemble` shape (the firm L1 cap §"L1 vs L0 distinction" / §Evidence, read at the L3 iteration-rotation tier). The L3 rendering (using the L3 value-thread vocabulary, with the per-term leaf delegated to the opaque libCEED kernel):

    -- L3 explicit integrator-fold: build the composite operator, loop the two integrator lists,
    -- accumulate one per-term sub-operator per iteration by AddSubOperator, finalize.
    fe_assemble_L3 :: (space, terms) -> K
    fe_assemble_L3 space terms =
      let (trial_restr, test_restr, trial_basis, test_basis) = build_fe_context space  -- 1. context hoist
      let op = make_composite_operator space        --    composite accumulator (ceed::Operator), zero-action
      let _  =                                       -- 2. positional for over the integrator lists
            for_each (domain_integs ++ boundary_integs) (\integ ->
              let sub_op = assemble_term_leaf integ trial_restr test_restr      -- libCEED leaf (opaque)
                                              trial_basis test_basis geom_data
              in op.AddSubOperator(sub_op))          -- 3. reduce by accumulation into the composite
      let _  = op.Finalize()                         --    finalize the composite (no-op at L1 algebra)
      in op  -- the composite operator's action == Σ_i assemble_term(space, term_i)

where:

- **`build_fe_context space`** is the L3 image of the space-capture hoist — the `CeedElemRestriction` / `CeedBasis` inputs Palace builds from the space (`bilinearform.cpp:64-70`: `trial_restr`/`test_restr` at 64/66, `trial_basis`/`test_basis` at 68/69), constructed *outside* the per-term accumulation. The L4 `readonly` once-captured `space` stratum dissolves into this explicit context construction; there is no type-level enforcement at L3 that the context is not rebuilt per term — it is a *coding convention* (the context sits outside the loop).
- **`make_composite_operator space`** is the composite `ceed::Operator` construction (`bilinearform.cpp:37-46`, the `SymmetricOperator` / general `ceed::Operator` construction by trial-test-coincidence) — the zero-action accumulator the per-term sub-operators reduce into; the L4 `zero` fold-identity.
- **`for_each (domain_integs ++ boundary_integs)`** is the **two** explicit `for (const auto &integ : domain_integs)` (`bilinearform.cpp:71-77`-region domain branch) / `for (const auto &integ : boundary_integs)` (`:87-97`-region boundary branch) loops — the L4 `foldr` over the single logical term list collapsed to two positional loops over the two L0 integrator containers (the `domain_integs ++ boundary_integs` concatenation the L1 cap names).
- **`assemble_term_leaf ...`** is the per-term `integ->Assemble(ceed, trial_restr, test_restr, trial_basis, test_basis, geom_data, geom_data_restr, &sub_op)` call (`bilinearform.cpp:75`) — the pure-virtual dispatch (`palace/fem/integrator.hpp:58-61`) into the **libCEED element-local quadrature kernel**. This theme treats it as one opaque per-term library call (the `obstruction (opaque-library-ownership)` sub-leaf, §"The opaque per-term sub-leaf"); it does NOT render the libCEED interior.
- **`op.AddSubOperator(sub_op)`** (`bilinearform.cpp:77` domain / `:97` boundary) is the L0 realization of the L4 fold's `Σ_i` reduction — the composite operator's action is the sum of the per-term sub-operator actions.
- **`op.Finalize()`** (`bilinearform.cpp:104`) finalizes the composite (an L0 representation step with no L1/L4 algebraic content).

The dissolution is **three** coordinated rewrites, one per piece of L4 assemble-fold-shell machinery — and each is a genuine vocabulary translation, NOT a rename:

### 1. Once-captured `readonly` space stratum → FE context construction hoisted outside the accumulation

The L4 once-captured `space : FiniteElementSpace[N]` (the `readonly` stratum bound outside the `foldr`) dissolves into the L3 `CeedElemRestriction` / `CeedBasis` context construction (`bilinearform.cpp:64-70`) placed *by hand* outside the per-term accumulation loops. This is the cap's space-capture law (the `space`-dependent assembly context is invariant across the fold and hoists out of it) realized as a coding convention: in Palace the restriction/basis inputs are built once and passed unchanged into every `integ->Assemble` call. The L4 type-level guarantee (the `readonly` stratum forbids per-term space mutation) dissolves into a coding convention at L3: the context sits outside the loop, but nothing at L3 type-enforces it. (This is the **same kind** of translation as the map sibling's operator-capture hoist — the context/operator-capture hoist is shared by the whole homomorphic family-loop branch; the two siblings differ in the *reduction shape* and the *leaf*, not the capture.)

### 2. Homomorphic map-then-reduce → explicit integrator-`for` + `AddSubOperator` accumulation

The L4 `foldr (\t acc -> assemble_term space t + acc) zero terms` dissolves into the L3 explicit positional `for` over the two integrator containers, each iteration building one `CeedOperator sub_op` (the per-term leaf) and **accumulating** it into the composite by `op->AddSubOperator(sub_op)` (`bilinearform.cpp:77` / `:97`). The combinator dissolution — how the per-element map body `\t -> assemble_term space t` becomes the L3 per-iteration `integ->Assemble(...)` leaf call, and how the `+ acc` reduction becomes the imperative `AddSubOperator` accumulation into a mutable composite — is the **map-then-reduce → imperative-accumulate translation**: the higher-order homomorphic `foldr` collapses to a first-order positional `for` that mutates a running composite operator. The single logical L4 term list dissolves into the **two** L0 integrator containers (`domain_integs` volume integrators, full-dimension geometry, `bilinearform.cpp:71-80`-region; `boundary_integs` surface integrators, dimension−1 geometry, `:84-103`-region) — but the cap's Law 2 (the concatenation homomorphism) is exactly what licenses the two L0 sub-lists being one L1/L4 fold: their assembled sums add. The independence the homomorphism names survives at L3: each `integ->Assemble` builds its `sub_op` reading only the shared hoisted context + its own term, with no read of the running composite's accumulated entries (the contributions are computed independently, then summed).

### 3. Opaque per-term leaf + single logical list → the libCEED `integ->Assemble` boundary + the domain/boundary two-list split

The L4 opaque quantified-over `assemble_term` dissolves into the concrete per-term library CALL `integ->Assemble(...)` (`bilinearform.cpp:75`) — the pure-virtual dispatch (`palace/fem/integrator.hpp:58-61`) into the **libCEED element-local quadrature kernel**. At L4 the entry only names `assemble_term` by role and quantifies over it; at L3 the per-term body resolves to a concrete library boundary the theme records as an `obstruction (opaque-library-ownership)` sub-leaf (§"The opaque per-term sub-leaf"). And the L4 single logical `terms : [WeakFormTerm]` list dissolves into the **two** L0 integrator containers iterated by two separate loops (domain then boundary), which the concatenation homomorphism (rewrite 2) re-unifies as one fold. The opaque `assemble_term` → the `integ->Assemble` libCEED boundary, and the single list → the domain/boundary two-list split, are the third vocabulary shift.

### The opaque per-term sub-leaf

**Status:** `obstruction (opaque-library-ownership)`

The per-term body bottoms out in an **opaque element-local quadrature kernel that lives entirely outside Palace** — Palace's `integ->Assemble` (`bilinearform.cpp:75`) is a pure-virtual dispatch (`palace/fem/integrator.hpp:58-61`) that builds a `CeedOperator` encapsulating the element-local quadrature contraction (basis evaluation at quadrature points, geometric-factor / coefficient weighting, contract-back to element dofs); that contraction runs **inside libCEED**. The full-assemble materialization (`CeedOperatorFullAssemble`, `palace/fem/libceed/operator.cpp:455`) similarly extracts the assembled entries via the libCEED `CeedOperatorAssembleCOO` API — the numerical assembly is library-internal. This is the **`obstruction (opaque-library-ownership)`** sub-kind (CLAUDE.md §Methodology invariants "Obstruction themes have two sub-kinds"); it is **already recorded in full** by the L1>L0 annotation [`fe-assemble-libceed-boundary-obstruction`](../L1-L0/fe-assemble-libceed-boundary-obstruction.md) (`opaque-library-ownership`). The functionality IS available to Palace but ONLY through the libCEED boundary; Palace never exposes the per-term element-local kernel as a standalone callable; there is nothing for Palace to fix upstream. **The negative anchor is Palace's CALL `integ->Assemble(...)` (`bilinearform.cpp:75`), NOT libCEED internals** — the obstruction is recorded at the Palace/libCEED ownership boundary, and this theme does not (and cannot) cite or lower the libCEED kernel interior. The promotion route is NONE in the conventional sense (Palace will not re-architect its libCEED consumption — libCEED is the matrix-free quadrature engine Palace is built on).

This makes the assemble dissolution **distinct from the map sibling** at the per-element level: the map's per-member solve (`ksp.Mult`) delegates to the firm [`ksp-solve-driver-dissolution`](./ksp-solve-driver-dissolution.md) (the per-member body *does* lower); the assemble's per-term body does NOT lower — it is an opaque library leaf the theme records (via the existing L1>L0 obstruction annotation), not delegates. The whole-theme status remains `firm` on the *outer-fold* structural rotation (the integrator-`for` + `AddSubOperator` accumulation + the context hoist, all read off positive source); only the per-term leaf is the opaque-library obstruction. This is the **same scoping** the carry-threaded sibling [`fold-solve-time-step-dissolution`](./fold-solve-time-step-dissolution.md) uses for its `ode->Step` per-step leaf (firm on the outer sweep, opaque leaf recorded).

### The map-not-fold distinction (the map-not-fold guard)

**This theme is the HOMOMORPHIC family-loop dissolution (the [`solve-family-map-dissolution`](./solve-family-map-dissolution.md) shape), NOT the carry-threaded [`fold-solve-time-step-dissolution`](./fold-solve-time-step-dissolution.md) shape — do NOT conflate** (the map-not-fold guard, OQ `l4-assemble-fold-combinator-scoping`). The L1/L4 `fe_assemble` cap is *written* with `foldr`, but the load-bearing semantic is that it is a **list-homomorphism producing a SUM**: each per-term contribution `assemble_term space t` is computed **independently of the running accumulator** (the cap's Law 2 concatenation homomorphism + Law 4 term-position commutativity), so the "fold" is a **map-then-reduce**, exactly the shape `solve_family`'s `map` is. The structural witnesses:

- **No carry-dependence.** No term's `assemble_term` reads the running operator sum `acc` to compute its contribution — `acc` is only the reduction target. Contrast `fold_solve`, where `time_step_op op s t` reads the prior carry `s` (the field-state) to advance it. `fe_assemble` has the map sibling's element-independence; `fold_solve` has the genuine fold's carry-threading.
- **NO `sequential-obstruction` on the term loop.** Because the contributions commute (Law 4) and are independent, the integrator-`for` is an **embarrassingly-parallel reduction written sequentially** (Palace happens to use one `Ceed` per OMP thread, `bilinearform.cpp:50-105`-region — the parallelism the homomorphism licenses is *exploited* here, unlike the map sibling's sequential `for`). It carries no read-after-write across iterations on the accumulated entries. Contrast `fold_solve`'s time-sweep, which carries a genuine `sequential-obstruction` (each step reads the prior step's in-place write to `sol`).

So `fe_assemble` is the **homomorphic sibling of `solve_family`** at the assemble half of the FE cohort. The single load-bearing difference from `solve-family-map-dissolution` is (a) the reduction shape — `solve_family` *collects* into a `std::vector<Vector>` slot (order-preserving), `fe_assemble` *reduces* by operator-`+` into a composite (order-independent); and (b) the leaf — `solve_family`'s per-member solve lowers, `fe_assemble`'s per-term leaf is the libCEED opaque-library boundary. Both share the once-captured `readonly` context hoist and the NO-carry / NO-`sequential-obstruction` family-loop semantics.

### What does NOT change in the rotation

The **per-term dataflow position** survives the rotation unchanged — each term's "assemble one weak-form contribution" passes through unchanged in dataflow position (one opaque leaf call per term, reduced into the sum); the rotation touches only the **fold-shell vocabulary**: the `readonly` once-captured `space` stratum becomes the hand-hoisted FE context construction, the homomorphic `foldr`-sum becomes the explicit integrator-`for` + `AddSubOperator` accumulation, the single logical term list becomes the domain/boundary two-list split re-unified by the homomorphism, the opaque `assemble_term` becomes the `integ->Assemble` libCEED boundary. The **term-independence** survives at L3 (the cap's Law 2 / Law 4): each `integ->Assemble` reads only the shared hoisted context + its own term, no cross-term coupling — the embarrassing-parallelism the concatenation-homomorphism licenses is *preserved* (and here exploited, one `Ceed` per thread). The term loop carries **NO `sequential-obstruction`** (the load-bearing contrast with `fold_solve`'s carry-threaded sweep).

### What this lowering does NOT cover

- **The per-term leaf interior** — it is the `obstruction (opaque-library-ownership)` sub-leaf (§"The opaque per-term sub-leaf"), recorded in full by [`fe-assemble-libceed-boundary-obstruction`](../L1-L0/fe-assemble-libceed-boundary-obstruction.md). The theme records the per-term body as the opaque `integ->Assemble` libCEED boundary (`bilinearform.cpp:75`) and does NOT lower the libCEED element-local kernel interior (out of scope per CLAUDE.md §Target-system — "cite Palace source, not vendored upstream").
- **The PA-vs-FA representation dispatch.** `BilinearForm::Assemble` (`bilinearform.cpp:141-151`) selects matrix-free partial-assembly vs materialized full-assembly by polynomial order (`UseFullAssembly`, `:115-138`). Both compute the same operator *action*; the representation choice is a Palace-owned variant axis on the firm fold (already absorbed at L1, the L1 cap §"assembly-representation" axis), not part of this fold-shell rotation. The COO→CSR materialization (`CeedOperatorFullAssemble`, `palace/fem/libceed/operator.cpp:455`) is the `full` realization of the leaf, recorded by the L1>L0 obstruction annotation.
- **BC-elimination.** Pinning essential dofs (`eliminate_essential_bc`) and lifting Dirichlet data into the RHS (`eliminate_rhs`) are **separable post-compositions** on the assembled operator (the L1 cap §"Algebraic laws" — explicitly NOT laws of `fe_assemble`), valid independently of how it was assembled. They are the firm L4 post-assembly BC-application verb-pair [`eliminate_bc`](../L4/eliminate_bc.md) (a separable post-composition pair), lowered by the sibling [`bc-elimination-post-composition-dissolution`](./bc-elimination-post-composition-dissolution.md) theme, NOT part of this assemble-fold dissolution.
- **The L3>L2 hop.** There is **no standalone `L3/fe_assemble` entry and no `L3-L2/fe-assemble-*` theme warranted** — see §"L3-entry-vs-dissolution-home verdict". The term-list fold carries **no `sequential-obstruction`** (independent terms; the reduction lifts), so it has no L3 obstruction to render as a distinct chapter and none to *erase* at an L3>L2 hop. This matches the map sibling's NO-ENTRY verdict (a standalone `L3/fe_assemble` would be a degenerate identity-in-named-terms restatement, the §1d smell). This L4>L3 theme is the **authoritative downward home** for the assemble-fold shell.

## L3-entry-vs-dissolution-home verdict

**WARRANT-FIRST per the vocabulary-shift redirect.** Both dispositions were considered; **the verdict is DISSOLUTION-HOME (no interposed `L3/fe_assemble` entry)** — analogous to [`solve-family-map-dissolution`](./solve-family-map-dissolution.md)'s NO-ENTRY verdict.

The decision criterion (per the `fold_solve` precedent, which went the OTHER way to L3-ENTRY): **does the L3 image carry a `sequential-obstruction` or `partial-obstruction` that warrants a standing iteration-rotation chapter?**

- **`fold_solve` got an L3 entry** (`partial-obstruction`) because its time-sweep carries BOTH a carry-threading `sequential-obstruction` AND an opaque per-step body — a genuine iteration-rotation the L3 layer must home (the [`chebyshev`](../L3/chebyshev.md) shape).
- **`solve_family` got NO L3 entry** because its family-map loop carries NO `sequential-obstruction` (independent members; the loop lifts) — a standalone L3 chapter would mirror the dissolution's RHS (the §1d anti-mirror smell).
- **`fe_assemble` matches the `solve_family` case.** The term-list fold carries **NO `sequential-obstruction`** (term-position commutativity, the cap's Law 4; the independent reduction lifts — indeed it is *exploited* in parallel, one `Ceed` per OMP thread). The ONLY obstruction is the **per-term leaf**, which is already homed by the L1>L0 `obstruction (opaque-library-ownership)` annotation (it is NOT a loop-structure obstruction the L3 iteration-rotation tier would render). So the L3 image of the assemble-fold shell is fully expressed by this theme's §"L3 form (RHS)"; a standalone `L3/fe_assemble` chapter would be a degenerate identity-in-named-terms mirror of that RHS (the §1d smell). This theme is the **authoritative L3-form home** for the assemble-fold shell, with the leaf-opacity already recorded one layer below.

**Both verdicts recorded as required, with the chosen one stated:** the L3-ENTRY disposition is VALID *in principle* (a reader navigating L3 could want a `fe_assemble` chapter) but is **rejected here** because it would be a mirror with no obstruction to render — the dissolution-home disposition is chosen. This is **re-openable** ONLY if a genuine NON-independent iteration surfaces in the assemble path that the homomorphism does not capture (none has; the libCEED leaf is library-owned, not a Palace loop). The NO-L2 warrant the `fe_assemble` L1 cap carries (it is a fold leaf with no other firm L1 dependency; the homomorphism is the whole structure) reinforces this: there is no L2 fusion-rotation content to interpose either, so the L4>L3 theme + the L1>L0 leaf-obstruction are the two substantive lowering homes, with no interposed L3/L2 chapters.

## Applicability conditions

The rewrite is valid when all four of the following hold (the first three are the homomorphic-fold-shell conditions; the fourth is the leaf-ownership condition):

1. **The finite-element space is captured once, shared across the term list.** `space : FiniteElementSpace[N]` is bound once, outside the `foldr`, and threaded unchanged into every per-term `assemble_term` — the `readonly` once-captured stratum (the firm cap §Signature). This is what lets the L3 FE context construction (`CeedElemRestriction` / `CeedBasis`, `bilinearform.cpp:64-70`) hoist outside the integrator loops.

2. **The per-term contributions are independent — no carry-dependence (the homomorphism / map-not-fold condition).** Each term's `assemble_term space t` is computed without reading the running operator sum; the reduction is by operator-`+` (commutative, associative — the cap's Law 2 + Law 4). 3. **The reduction has an identity (the empty-term zero operator).** `fe_assemble space [] = zero` (the cap's Law 1); the L3 form realizes this as the composite operator constructed with no sub-operators added then finalized (`bilinearform.cpp:43-46`-region empty-thread + the guarded `!domain_integs.empty()` / `!boundary_integs.empty()` branches). The zero-action operator is the fold identity.

4. **The per-term leaf is an opaque library-owned kernel the combinator quantifies over.** `assemble_term` is not rendered at L4; at L3 it resolves to a single library CALL (`integ->Assemble` → libCEED). This is what licenses the `obstruction (opaque-library-ownership)` per-term sub-leaf treatment (§"The opaque per-term sub-leaf"); if the per-term body were a Palace-authored loop it would lower instead of being recorded as an opaque leaf. The boundary is identical across all 5 solver pipelines (every pipeline assembles through this same `BilinearForm` / `CeedOperator` machinery — the L1>L0 obstruction annotation §"Applicability conditions" establishes this), so this theme's leaf-treatment covers FE assembly for all pipelines.
