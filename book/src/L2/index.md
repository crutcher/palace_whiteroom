# L2 — Algebraic decompositions

The canonical algebraic decomposition: each operation written as composition of base tensor / operator / quadrature primitives, with HPC/SIMD optimization tricks **unfolded back into the base algebras**. The **fusion rotation** layer.

## Context

L2 is the layer where:
- Cache-blocked loops, SIMD intrinsics, manual unrolling are erased — they are below L2's level of abstraction.
- Kernel fusion across multiple algebraic operations is unfolded into composition.
- Packed sparse formats are de-packed to dense/symbolic algebraic operators.
- Batched specialized BLAS calls are written as compositions of base primitives.

**Load-bearing numerical tricks** (non-associative reduction orderings, fast-math, mixed-precision intermediates, deterministic-vs-atomic accumulation) are **preserved as explicit algebraic claims** with the property they buy called out.

## Semantics (overlay)

L2 vocabulary: tensors, linear operators, quadrature rules, basis transformations, primitive operations (axpy, dot, matvec, gemv, trsv, scal, nrm2, …). State threading via explicit value semantics. Compositions of L1 primitives into method-step shapes are first-class at L2.

Two L2 vocabulary **motifs** organize the firm cohort:

- **Named compositions** — a single Palace runtime-dispatched entry point unfolds into a canonical pipeline of L1 leaves under a named L2 surface. Two firm exemplars: (i) `orthogonalize` names the Gram-Schmidt `project ▷ subtract` composition (`dot` ▷ `axpy`), turning the opaque `gs_orthog ∈ {MGS, CGS, CGS2}` parameter into the visible per-variant batching / sequencing disclosed as the collective-shape residual axis — the level-(b)-absorbed `op.orthog` surface `krylov_step` folds; (ii) `incremental_least_squares` names the GMRES / FGMRES running-QR / Givens-rotation-stream small-dense LS update — the `replay ▷ generate ▷ apply ▷ apply_rhs` ▷ terminal-back-solve pipeline with FIXED sub-step sequence (replay-before-generate non-commutative load-bearing) and the running residual `β = |s[j+1]|` as a free byproduct. The outer-driver `ksp_solve` (firm) is the third named composition — wrapping the `krylov_step` kernel in the convergence-test / restart `iterate_while` fold — distinct from the first two by sitting one layer up (composes over `krylov_step` itself, not its L1 primitives). The shift-invert `eigsolve` (firm) is the fourth — `apply_linop(M) ▷ ksp_solve((K−σM)⁻¹)` per-step body composed over a constructed inverse solver, with the eigen-iteration *fold* opaque-library-owned (the load-bearing reason its L3 backfill lands `partial-obstruction`).
- **Fold cohorts** — variadic reductions sharing a `foldl` skeleton but **distinct homomorphisms**, kept as separate L2 entries with an explicit *do-NOT-merge* boundary. The two folds:
  - `inner_product` folds the **length axis** to a `Scalar` (`foldl (+) zero (zipWith kernel x y)`) — the conjugation/element-type/weight specializations of `dot` / `tdot` / `bilinear_form`.
  - `linear_combination` folds the **term axis**, keeping `Tensor[N]` (`foldl (\acc (a,t) -> acc + a·t) zeros pairs`) — the arity specializations of `scal` / `axpy` / `axpby` / `axpbypcz`.

  They share the fold skeleton but target different codomains (`Scalar` vs `Tensor[N]`); merging them would erase the codomain distinction and the do-NOT-merge note carried in both entries' dep-map rows is load-bearing.
- **Combinator-as-entry + specialization/consumer stubs** — for the BLAS-1 fold families, the L2 entry for the family is the **fold combinator** (`linear_combination`, `inner_product`); the once-standalone same-named leaves are **specialization / consumer pointers** up to the combinator carrying only the leaf-level facts the combinator does not (the leaf's unique L0 anchors + its one variant-axis row). This is the **vocabulary-shift-redirect** model: the combinator is the home, the leaf is a thin specialization pointer. It **supersedes** the retired "rectangular floor / leaf-vs-fold fork" framing — a same-named base-form floor mirrored beside the combinator was the rectangular pattern the redirect supersedes. The fold-family members:
  - **Specializations of `linear_combination`**: `scal` (arity-1), `axpy` (arity-2, second coeff 1), `axpby` (arity-2 general), `axpbypcz` (arity-3) — each `scal(α,x) = linear_combination [(α,x)]` etc., the family member at a fixed arity; the duplicated semantics/laws/fusion body is collapsed into the combinator, the member keeps its unique L0 anchors + the output-aliasing variant row (which is the **FOLD's** axis).
  - **Specialization + consumer of `inner_product`**: `dot` is the **`M = I` Hermitian/symmetric specialization** of `inner_product` (the conjugation variant-axis — `dot` Hermitian vs `tdot` unconjugated — is the value-bearing leaf-level fact the stub keeps); `nrm2` is the **consumer** `√ ∘ abs ∘ inner_product` at `y=x` (NOT a fold member — do-NOT-merge; the `std::abs` defensive guard preserved as an explicit load-bearing numerical claim). The do-NOT-merge §"Fold-cohort boundary" remains load-bearing.
- **Standalone elementwise / gate floors** (`reciprocal`, `elementwise_product`, `assemble_diagonal`, `jacobi-smoother`, `divfree_projector`) — same-named L2 entries under firm L3 entries with **NO fold-parent**: standalone elementwise leaves (`reciprocal` the nonlinear multiplicative-inverse self-map; `elementwise_product` the Hadamard binary op) and constructed-operator / operator-to-data gates (`assemble_diagonal` the operator-to-data sibling of `apply_linop`; `jacobi-smoother` the thinnest constructed-operator gate; `divfree_projector` the four-step Helmholtz gate). Four are thin identity-in-form entries; `divfree_projector` carries one genuine `AddMult` apply-accumulate de-fusion. Having no fold combinator to point up to, these stay full standalone entries (no specialization-stub reduction applies — there is no combinator-as-entry to defer to).

## Vocabulary cohort

**Firm at L2** (algebraic-laws + variant-axis-coverage complete):

- `krylov_step` — the recurring Krylov/polynomial step kernel (kernel half of the kernel-plus-driver shape; the driver half is L4 `iterate_while`).
- `chebyshev-iteration` — the three-term polynomial recurrence; concrete L2 entry behind `krylov_step` variant-axis 3. **A `correction_step` specialization** choosing `B = p_order(D⁻¹A)`.
- `correction_step` — step-kernel combinator; the preconditioned residual-correction step `y + B·(x − A·y)`, the shared per-sweep body across the smoother family + GMG V-cycle (named verbatim in Palace's own comments). The combinator-as-entry: `jacobi-smoother` (`B=ω·D⁻¹`) and `chebyshev-iteration` (`B=p_order(D⁻¹A)`) are `B`-choosing specializations; the conjugated `B=T·B'·Tᵀ` is the distributive/coarse-grid choice (one combinator, conjugation a `B`-choice).
- `linear_combination` — fold-cohort, reduce-to-`Tensor[N]`; the BLAS-1 scalar-weighted-sum arity family (`scal`/`axpy`/`axpby`/`axpbypcz`).
- `inner_product` — fold-cohort, reduce-to-`Scalar`; the BLAS-1 inner-product conjugation family (`dot`/`tdot`/`bilinear_form`); sibling fold of `linear_combination` (do-NOT-merge).
- `orthogonalize` — named-composition, `project ▷ subtract`; the Gram-Schmidt `op.orthog` surface `krylov_step` folds.
- `ksp_solve` — named-composition (**outer-driver** wrap above the firm L1 `ksp_solve`): the restart / convergence-test loop that folds the `krylov_step` kernel into a complete solve. Establishes the **non-identity** L2↔L1 relationship (distinct from the identity `L3>L2` `krylov-step-body-identity` theme).
- `gram` — the **all-pairs `inner_product` fold** → `Matrix[k,k]`: the matrix-valued lift of the `inner_product` scalar fold, building the `k×k` Gram `XᴴX` from a `k`-column basis `X`. Hermitian + PSD (PD iff `X` full-rank); the incremental-Gram block law certifies the fold over columns. Consumed by `deflate` (the oblique-projection assembly that LU-solves the Gram).cpp:524-531`).
- `incremental_least_squares` — the GMRES / FGMRES running-QR / Givens-stream small-dense LS update; the **second named composition** (sibling to `orthogonalize`) closing the `l2-named-composition-lifts` cohort. Running-QR fan-down `replay ▷ generate ▷ apply ▷ apply_rhs` with terminal `back_solve`; FIXED sub-step sequence (replay-before-generate non-commutative load-bearing); two parametric axes `basis_kind ∈ {V, Z}` + `variant ∈ {real, complex}`; running residual `β = |s[j+1]|` is a free byproduct.
- `eigsolve` — **shift-invert spectral-transform application** `apply_shift_invert = apply_linop(M) ▷ ksp_solve((K − σM)⁻¹)` (the per-step body the opaque-library eigen-iteration folds). Chain-step-2 of the eigsolve prerequisite chain (L1-firm → this entry → L3-backfill); first L2 named composition whose direct constituent is itself a constructed-solver composition. The per-step body is opened; the eigen-iteration *fold* stays library-owned (SLEPc `EPSSolve` / ARPACK RCI) — the inverse decomposition from `ksp_solve` whose fold IS opened.

*Fold-family specializations / consumers (the combinator is the entry, these are thin specialization/consumer pointers — NOT standalone floors; each defers all semantics/laws/fusion content to its combinator and keeps only its unique L0 anchors + its one variant-axis row):*

- `dot` — **`M = I` Hermitian/symmetric specialization-stub** of `inner_product`; the conjugation variant-axis (`dot` Hermitian-conjugated vs `tdot` unconjugated — value-bearing for complex vectors) is the leaf-level fact the stub keeps. Do-NOT-merge (§"Fold-cohort boundary").
- `nrm2` — **consumer-stub** of `inner_product` (`√ ∘ abs ∘ inner_product` at `y=x`, NOT a fold member — do-NOT-merge); the `std::abs` defensive guard preserved as an explicit load-bearing numerical claim.
- `scal` — **arity-1 specialization-stub** of `linear_combination` (`scal(α,x) = linear_combination [(α,x)]`).
- `axpy` — **arity-2 specialization-stub** of `linear_combination` (`axpy(α,x,y) = linear_combination [(α,x),(1,y)]`, second coefficient fixed to 1); the output-aliasing variant axis is the **fold's**, not leaf-specific.
- `axpby` — **arity-2 specialization-stub** of `linear_combination` (`axpby(α,x,β,y) = linear_combination [(α,x),(β,y)]`); output-aliasing axis is the fold's.
- `axpbypcz` — **arity-3 specialization-stub** of `linear_combination` (`axpbypcz(α,x,β,y,γ,z) = linear_combination [(α,x),(β,y),(γ,z)]`); output-aliasing axis is the fold's.

*Fused composite (NO fold combinator to defer to — stays a full standalone entry):*

- `normalize` — the fused vector-normalisation composite `normalize(x) = (nrm2(x), scal(1/nrm2(x), x))`, returning **both** the norm and the unit vector. **NO fold-parent** — neither a member of `inner_product` (its codomain is the unit `Tensor[N]` alongside the norm `Scalar`, not a bare `Scalar`) nor of `linear_combination` (the fused norm-then-rescale pairing is not a term-axis scalar-weighted-sum). Cites the same-layer L2 `nrm2` + `scal` stubs as *consumed* constituents. Thin / identity-in-form (no genuine kernel fusion to unfold — `linalg::Normalize` is already the one-line norm-then-rescale composition); the partiality non-law at `x=0` and the IEEE-754 reduction-tree caveats transport unchanged.

*Standalone elementwise / gate entries (same-named L2 entries under firm L3 entries with **NO fold combinator** to defer to — each is a standalone elementwise leaf or constructed-operator / operator-to-data gate; they stay full standalone entries, no specialization-stub reduction applies):*

- `reciprocal` — standalone elementwise multiplicative-inverse leaf (`y[i] = 1/x[i]`); **NO fold-parent** (a *nonlinear* self-map, `1/(a+b) ≠ 1/a + 1/b`). Thin identity-in-form floor, eight laws + single element-type axis inherited unchanged from L1.
- `elementwise_product` — standalone Hadamard binary field op (`a ⊙ b`); **NO fold-parent** (a binary `(Tensor[N], Tensor[N]) -> Tensor[N]` op, neither reduce-to-`Scalar` nor reduce-to-`Tensor[N]`; the inverse-subsumption generalisation of `scal`). Thin identity-in-form floor, ten laws + element-type / conjugation axes.
- `assemble_diagonal` — standalone operator-to-data diagonal-extraction primitive (`A → diag(A)`); **NO fold-parent**; the operator-to-data sibling of `apply_linop` (NOT an `apply_linop` variant — operator/data divide load-bearing). Thin floor, **load-bearing matrix-free high-order-Nedelec approximate-diagonal non-law preserved through the floor** (so `firm`, not `partly-constructive`).
- `jacobi-smoother` — the **thinnest** constructed-operator gate (apply `op.dinv ⊙ x = (ω·D⁻¹) ⊙ x`, one elementwise product); **NO fold-parent**; the fusion rotation is **negative** (no fused multi-operation kernel to unfold, unlike `chebyshev-iteration`). Degree-zero member of the `chebyshev-iteration` recurrence (cited, not dep).
- `divfree_projector` — the divergence-free Helmholtz-projection constructed-operator gate (fixed four-step `WeakDiv → Z_{bdr_eff} → ksp_solve → Grad`); **NO fold-parent**; a **moderate** floor — one genuine `AddMult` apply-accumulate de-fusion (step-4 `Grad->AddMult` → `apply_linop ▷ axpy`), all else identity-in-form; direct L2 dep `ksp_solve` (the inner projected solve carrying its `sequential-obstruction` **by reference**).

*Constructive-kernel compositions (a NEW L2 cohort kind — named contraction-chain compositions of the element-local substrate over the rank-structured `concepts/element-local-tensor` family, the burn/GPU matrix-free backend-lowering surface; distinct from the flat-`Tensor[N]` BLAS-1 fold / named-composition / gate cohorts):*

- `matrix-free-operator-apply` — the named contraction-chain combinator for matrix-free FE operator application `A = Gᵀ ∘ B_𝒟ᵀ ∘ D ∘ B_𝒟 ∘ G`, composing the four firm element-local substrate ops by name (`element_restrict` = G/Gᵀ, `basis_apply` = B_𝒟/B_𝒟ᵀ, `quad_point_contract` = D, `geom_factor_build` = the geom carrier) over the `concepts/element-local-tensor` shapes. Composition-level laws: linearity of the apply; the `Gᵀ…G` self-adjoint/symmetry sandwich (transports the pointwise `D` symmetry to the global operator → de-Rham SPD); element-additivity of the `Gᵀ` scatter-add (the matrix-free analog of FE element-matrix assembly). The L1 [`libceed-quadrature-kernel-impl`](../L1/libceed-quadrature-kernel-impl.md) is its concrete realization (identity-in-named-terms downward — in-line note, NO L2-L1 theme); the `fe_assemble` fold sums it per-term. Sum-factorization is a transparent performance trick (one note); the assembled-COO form is the derived materialization (one note).

**Partly-constructive at L2** (firm structural decomposition + a constructive sub-part with a stated promotion condition):

- `deflate` — the **oblique / Galerkin complementary projector** `I − X(XᴴX)⁻¹Xᴴ` removing the deflation subspace `span(X)`; the `coords ▷ schur-solve ▷ back-project` named composition over `gram` + `lu_solve` + `linear_combination` + `dot`. The **Schur-form pipeline** is firm on the positive `deflated_solve` site (`nleps.cpp:505-537`); the **bare-Galerkin core** `I − X(XᴴX)⁻¹Xᴴ` (the `S = I` degenerate case) is the constructive sub-part, materialized from the deflation-scheme literature + the negative anchor (no bare-Gram solve appears outside the Schur-wrapped block). Promotion to firm gates on a positive Palace Galerkin-deflation source site. **Over-unification guard:** NOT the same combinator as `orthogonalize` — `orthogonalize` is `deflate` at `gram = I` (orthonormal basis, Gram-solve-free sequential subtraction); the `(XᴴX)⁻¹` Gram solve is load-bearing.

## Working Notes

- This is the layer most populated by `combinator-miner` output — recurrent patterns are L2 candidates.
- `krylov_step` carries six variant axes and one non-trivial algebraic law (the demand-pruning law over `outputs` extras, inherited from `derived-view-hoisting`); the kernel's non-laws (commutativity, associativity, fold-merge, step-composition, linearity, bit-determinism-across-variants) are catalogued explicitly. Consumed by the L4 `iterate_while` + `solve-monad` outer driver (firm `book/src/L4/krylov_step.md` Form A/Form B for CG; firm L0 Sub-pattern C for GMRES/Arnoldi; `book/src/L4/chebyshev.md`). Five pattern instances: CG step bodies (`book/src/L4/krylov_step.md` Form B + L0 Sub-pattern B, `iterative.cpp:360-486`); GMRES `inner_loop` (L0 Sub-pattern C, `iterative.cpp:543-705`); `book/src/L4/chebyshev.md` §Semantics `innerStep`; Arnoldi step (L0 Sub-pattern C inner Arnoldi loop within `iterative.cpp:563-683`); `concepts/negative-result-slice.md` §Partial-positive sub-pattern.
- **MINRES/BiCGStab obstruction-theme guidance**: the MINRES and BiCGStab L1>L0 themes (`book/src/L1-L0/minres-iteration.md`, `book/src/L1-L0/bicgstab-iteration.md`) sketch speculative L1 operators (`lanczos_step`, `three_term_recurrence_update`, `givens_apply_with_residual_min`, `bicgstab_step`, `omega_update`, `stabilisation_update`); none is promoted to firm L1 — each is a *step-body specialisation* of `krylov_step`, not an orthogonal axis that would simplify its L2 semantics (decision at `scaffolding/decisions/2026-05-27-krylov-step-speculative-l1-promotion.md`).
- **Fold-cohort boundary (load-bearing, do NOT merge).** `inner_product` and `linear_combination` share a `foldl` skeleton but are distinct homomorphisms targeting different codomains: `inner_product` folds the **length axis** to `Scalar`; `linear_combination` folds the **term axis**, keeping `Tensor[N]`. The do-NOT-merge note is carried in both dep-map rows and in each entry's §"Sibling fold". `nrm2` / `matrix_weighted_norm` is a *consumer* of `inner_product` (`√ ∘ inner_product` at `y=x`), not an instance.
  - **Combinator-as-entry + specialization-stub leaves.** The fold family's L2 entry is the **combinator** (`inner_product` / `linear_combination`); the once-standalone same-named leaves are specialization / consumer pointers that defer all semantics/laws/fusion content to the combinator and keep only their unique L0 anchors + one variant-axis row. A same-named base-form floor mirrored beside the combinator is the retired rectangular pattern the vocabulary-shift redirect supersedes. The do-NOT-merge boundary keeps `dot` a *specialization* and `nrm2` a *consumer* of `inner_product` rather than merged into it. The `linear_combination` arity members (`scal`/`axpy`/`axpby`/`axpbypcz`) and the `inner_product` reduce-family members (`dot`/`nrm2`) are folded into their combinators' §Specializations / §Consumer (current count: 17 firm + 1 partly-constructive = 18 rows — see :95).
- **L2 kernel/driver pair.** [`ksp_solve`](./ksp_solve.md) is the **outer-driver** wrap above the firm L1 [`ksp_solve`](../L1/ksp_solve.md): the restart / convergence-test `iterate_while` fold of the [`krylov_step`](./krylov_step.md) kernel into a complete solve. It establishes the substantive **non-identity** L2↔L1 rotation (the L1 solver-as-operator opacity is opened into the kernel-fold composition while the iteration view stays erased) — distinct from the identity [`krylov-step-body-identity`](../L3-L2/krylov-step-body-identity.md) kernel theme; the companion lowering is the firm L3>L2 theme [`ksp-solve-outer-driver`](../L3-L2/ksp-solve-outer-driver.md). The L2 `ksp_solve` outer-driver wrap and the L3 [`krylov_step`](../L3/krylov_step.md) kernel form a driver/kernel pair across the L2↔L3 boundary, mirroring the L2-kernel / L4-driver pair (`krylov_step` at L2, `iterate_while` at L4).
- **Gram/deflate fold-lift + eigsolve.** [`gram`](./gram.md) is the **all-pairs `inner_product` fold → `Matrix[k,k]`** (firm-on-positive-structure on the Gram-build site `nleps.cpp:524-531`); Hermitian + PSD, consumed by `deflate`. [`deflate`](./deflate.md) (the first L2 `partly-constructive` entry) is the oblique/Galerkin complementary projector `I − X(XᴴX)⁻¹Xᴴ`, the `coords ▷ schur-solve ▷ back-project` named composition over `gram` + [`lu_solve`](../L1/lu_solve.md) + `linear_combination` + `dot`; the Schur-form pipeline is firm (positive `deflated_solve` site `nleps.cpp:505-537`), the bare-Galerkin core (`S = I`) is the constructive sub-part (promotion-gated on a positive Palace Galerkin-deflation site). Over-unification guard: `deflate` is NOT `orthogonalize` (`orthogonalize` = `deflate` at `gram = I`; the `(XᴴX)⁻¹` Gram solve is load-bearing). [`eigsolve`](./eigsolve.md) is the **named shift-invert spectral-transform composition** `apply_shift_invert = apply_linop(M) ▷ ksp_solve((K − σM)⁻¹)`; the per-step body is opened but the eigen-iteration fold is opaque-library-owned (SLEPc `EPSSolve` / ARPACK RCI), the load-bearing reason the L3 backfill landed `partial-obstruction` (see [`L3/eigsolve`](../L3/eigsolve.md)).
- **Standalone elementwise / gate floors (fork-INDEPENDENT, NO fold-parent).** [`reciprocal`](./reciprocal.md) (elementwise multiplicative-inverse leaf, nonlinear self-map); [`elementwise_product`](./elementwise_product.md) (Hadamard binary field op, the inverse-subsumption generalisation of `scal`); [`assemble_diagonal`](./assemble_diagonal.md) (operator-to-data diagonal-extraction, NOT an `apply_linop` variant — firm not partly-constructive, the load-bearing matrix-free high-order-Nedelec approximate-diagonal non-law preserved through the floor); [`jacobi-smoother`](./jacobi-smoother.md) (the thinnest constructed-operator gate, fusion rotation **negative**, degree-zero member of the `chebyshev-iteration` recurrence); [`divfree_projector`](./divfree_projector.md) (the divergence-free Helmholtz-projection gate, one genuine `AddMult` apply-accumulate de-fusion, direct L2 dep [`ksp_solve`](./ksp_solve.md) carrying that gate's `sequential-obstruction` by reference); [`normalize`](./normalize.md) (the fused composite `nrm2 ∘ scal` returning both the norm and the unit vector, a composite-with-no-fold-parent). Having no fold-parent, all are design-final under the leaf-vs-fold distinction.
- **`chebyshev` floor naming exception.** `chebyshev`'s L2 floor is present as the firm [`chebyshev-iteration`](./chebyshev-iteration.md) (`L3/chebyshev` lowers to it) under a non-same-named slug — a deliberate naming exception to the "same-named L2 floor" convention (a same-named `L2/chebyshev.md` pointer would be pure naming bureaucracy; the L3 reader already finds `chebyshev` floored).
