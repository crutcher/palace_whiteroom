# L3 — Global tensor-field operations

L2 algebraic decompositions re-expressed as global tensor-field / convolution-over-space operations: whole-tensor ops, no element loops. The **iteration rotation** layer.

## Context

Where the L2 algebra admits a global form, L3 captures it. Where no global form exists (Gauss-Seidel-flavored smoothers, certain triangular solves, sequentially-reordered preconditioners), the **obstruction** is recorded as a first-class output — negative L3 results are part of the deliverable.

## Semantics (overlay)

L3 expresses:
- **Whole-tensor field operations** — primitives that act on whole tensors with no element loop exposed at the layer's vocabulary, L3-native by signature shape. The closed BLAS-1 cohort: matvec (`apply_linop`, the linear-operator-application generalisation of "matvec" — square and rectangular operators, real and complex, all operator representations absorbed), the linear-update family (`linear_combination`, with `scal`/`axpy`/`axpby`/`axpbypcz` as its arity specializations), and the reductions (`dot`, `nrm2`).
- **Field transitions** — state evolution over a single algorithmic step expressed as `state' = f(state, params)`. The composition operator `krylov-step` is the canonical instance: the value-threaded per-step kernel `(op, K, s) -> (K', s', outputs)` built from the whole-tensor field operations above.
- **Convolution-like patterns** where applicable (stencil sweeps, restriction/prolongation).
- **Sequential obstructions** — explicit markers where global form is unavailable, with reason (recorded per `concepts/sequential-obstruction.md`). The L3 cohort arranges on an **obstruction-profile spectrum** from obstruction-free, through obstruction-carrying, to obstruction-authoring. At the **obstruction-free end** sit (i) the leaf / single-field-op gates ([`jacobi-smoother`](./jacobi-smoother.md), [`apply_linop`](./apply_linop.md), [`dot`](./inner_product.md#specializations), [`scal`](./linear_combination.md#arity-specializations), [`reciprocal`](./reciprocal.md), [`elementwise_product`](./elementwise_product.md) — apply is a single field op, no loop of any kind) and (ii) the **fused-composite-obstruction-free** profile, exemplified by [`normalize`](./normalize.md) (`firm`) — a fused composite (`nrm2 + scal`) that authors no loop because **both constituents are individually obstruction-free** (the `nrm2` reduction is parallel-clean, the `scal` rescale is embarrassingly parallel); the cleanest end of the spectrum (a composite, yet obstruction-free). Six non-trivial-obstruction shapes then coexist: (a) the outer loop folding `krylov-step` does not lift, but the firm driver [`ksp_solve`](./ksp_solve.md) still renders it as an explicit value-threaded tail recursion (firm); (b) [`chebyshev`](./chebyshev.md) (`partial-obstruction`) — body lifts, but the inner `k`-recurrence + outer `pc_it` Richardson sweep are sequential obstructions rooted in **numerical stability**, **unconditional** (holds for every parameter value); (c) [`eigsolve`](./eigsolve.md) (`partial-obstruction`) — body lifts, but the eigen-iteration loop is a sequential obstruction rooted in **opaque-library-ownership** (Palace authors no loop — SLEPc `EPSSolve` / ARPACK RCI own it), also **unconditional**; (d) [`divfree-projector`](./divfree-projector.md) (`firm`) — **obstruction-carrying by reference**: its own apply is a fixed obstruction-free straight-line composition (`WeakDiv → Z_{bdr_eff} → ksp_solve → Grad`) authoring no loop of its own, but step 3 invokes the firm-L3 [`ksp_solve`](./ksp_solve.md) whose outer fold IS a sequential-obstruction; the gate neither introduces a new obstruction nor erases the inner one (the inner iteration stays interior to `ksp_solve` per the [`nested-constructed-operator-gate`](../concepts/nested-constructed-operator-gate.md) fidelity rule); (e) [`orthogonalize`](./orthogonalize.md) (`partial-obstruction`) — body lifts (one `dot` + one `axpy` against whole tensors, all variants), but the obstruction is **variant-conditional**: the MGS branch of the `gs_orthog` axis is a Palace-authored per-column `sequential-obstruction` rooted in **numerical stability** (the serial rank-1-projector chain buys the roundoff-orthogonality the batched form loses), while the CGS/CGS2 branches **lift cleanly** to the batched global statements `H = Vᴴw` / `w' = w − VH`. Shape (e) is the **variant-conditional** sibling of (b): same numerical-stability obstruction *root* as `chebyshev`, but where (b)/(c) carry **unconditional** obstructions (every parameter value), (e)'s partial-obstruction verdict **splits along the `gs_orthog` variant axis** — a structure neither (b) nor (c) exhibits; (f) [`fold_solve`](./fold_solve.md) (`partial-obstruction`) — body lifts (one opaque whole-state advance `time_step_op op s t`), but the outer time-sweep does not lift for a **combined** reason: a **carry-threading** `sequential-obstruction` (each step's input is the prior step's in-place write to `sol`, the schedule does not commute) PLUS an **opaque-library per-step leaf** (the MFEM `ODESolver` step). Shape (f) is distinct from each precedent: unlike (b)/(c) its per-step body is an opaque leaf with **no** L2 composition, and unlike (c) **Palace authors the outer sweep** (`transientsolver.cpp:77`), so the loop RENDERS as an explicit tail recursion (the `ksp_solve`/`chebyshev` rendering, NOT `eigsolve`'s un-renderable case) — the obstruction is the carry-threading + opaque leaf, not the whole loop being library-owned. (b), (c), (e), and (f) are the four `partial-obstruction` operators (body-lifts-loop-doesn't); (a) and (d) are `firm` (their obstruction is rendered or carried-by-reference, not a partial lift). The distinction across the obstruction-carrying shapes is *whose* loop carries the obstruction and on *what condition*: `ksp_solve` its own (always), `eigsolve` an opaque library's (always), `divfree-projector` its inner `ksp_solve` gate's (by reference), `chebyshev` its own recurrence/sweep (always, numerical-stability), `orthogonalize` its own MGS column-recurrence (**only on the MGS variant**, numerical-stability), `fold_solve` its own Palace-authored sweep (always, **carry-threading + opaque per-step leaf** — the combined shape, the loop renders but does not lift).
