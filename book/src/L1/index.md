# L1 — Mutation-lifted forms

Source operations re-expressed as pure functions: explicit input set, output set; in-place mutation and aliasing patterns either erased (workspace/scratch buffers) or made explicit (semantically-meaningful aliasing). The **mutation rotation** layer.

## Context

L1 is the closest pure-functional layer to the source. Structure follows the source loop; what changes is:

- **In-place vector updates → fresh-value updates.** `y.Add(α, x)` and `y.AXPBY(α, x, β)` (mutating member methods) become `y_new = axpy(α, x, y_old)` and `y_new = axpby(α, x, β, y_old)`. The L0 destination buffer disappears from the signature; the L1>L0 lowering reintroduces it.
- **Receiver-vs-argument asymmetry → first-class conjugation argument.** `ComplexVector::Dot` is a method on `*this`, making the receiver the linear argument and the call argument the conjugated one. At L1 the method-form / free-function-form distinction is erased: `dot` is sesquilinear in fixed argument order (first argument conjugated).
- **Operator-application mutation → pure operator-as-function.** `A.Mult(x, y)` (writes into `y`) → `y = A·x` (no destination buffer mention). Pattern recurs in `apply_BA`, residuals, and B-weighted norms.
- **Pinned reduction tree → reduction as a single semantic step.** L0 `dot` and `nrm2` are layered as `Hypre per-rank kernel + MPI_Allreduce`; L1 names the reduction as one step and records floating-point reduction-tree non-associativity as a **load-bearing** algebraic claim (per `CLAUDE.md` "Optimization tricks vs. base algebra"), not as separate operators.
- **Iterative loop mutating iterate in place → functional unfold** `state_{k+1} = step(state_k)`. Workspace `tmp` is omitted (the COW backend handles allocation).
- **Construction-bound solver state → opaque type at the L1 surface.** `BaseKspSolver<OperType>::Mult(b, x)` (writes into `x`, dispatches through owned `IterativeSolver` + `Solver` `unique_ptr`s, mutates statistics counters, logs convergence warnings) becomes `(x, status) = ksp_solve(K, b)` where `K : Solver[A]` is opaque about its internal Krylov method, per-method workspace, and preconditioner representation. Per-method enum dispatch (CG / GMRES / FGMRES) is variant-absorbed; cumulative counters lift to driver-side accumulation over per-call `SolveResult.iterations`.

## Semantics (overlay)

L1 vocabulary mirrors the source operations but with pure-functional binding. Six semantic motifs recur across the firm operators:

1. **Element-wise pure update** (`axpy`, `axpby`) — element-local, reduction-free, every output element depends on exactly one input element from each tensor argument. Algebraic laws are linear-combination facts; constant-folding branches at L0 (e.g., `axpy`'s `α == 1.0` fast path) are transparent performance tricks that disappear at L1.
2. **Mutation-free reduction** (`dot`, `nrm2`) — reduction over the length axis to a scalar. Reduction-tree non-associativity is load-bearing and recorded as an explicit non-law; the MPI collective is folded into the L1>L0 lowering, not the L1 signature.
3. **Subsumption-as-identity rather than dependency** — when one operator is a specialisation of another (`axpy(α, x, y) = axpby(α, x, 1, y)`), both stay in the L1 dep-map as siblings; the relationship is captured by an algebraic law in the subsuming operator, not by a dep-map edge.
4. **Constructed-operator absorption** (`ksp_solve`, `eigsolve`, `chebyshev-smoother`, `divfree-projector`) — the L1 form takes a structured opaque `Solver[A]` (or `EigSolver`, `ChebSmoother`, `DivFreeProjector`) argument whose per-method body (CG / GMRES / FGMRES), preconditioner, tolerances, and iteration cap are bound at construction; the L1 signature is variant-free. Result is structured (`SolveResult` carries `x` + four solve-statistics fields) rather than the L0 in-place destination + side-effect logger + mutating counters. The L2 `krylov-step` operator is where the per-method body unfolds. The L1>L0 lowering — [`ksp-solve-mutation-rotation`](../L1-L0/ksp-solve-mutation-rotation.md) (cycle-008) — is the first L1>L0 theme for a structured opaque primary argument. It decomposes into the firm sister-theme primitives ([`apply-linop-mutation-rotation`](../L1-L0/apply-linop-mutation-rotation.md), [`axpby-mutation-rotation`](../L1-L0/axpby-mutation-rotation.md), [`axpbypcz-mutation-rotation`](../L1-L0/axpbypcz-mutation-rotation.md)) per-step plus four absorption rules (timer erase, warning-to-structured-field, counter-to-driver-accumulator, destination-binding) at the outer composition. This motif is *absorption-to-result*: the constructed argument is solved/iterated/applied-as-policy until it yields a structured value. A bare opaque-operator *action* — [`apply_linop`](./apply_linop.md)'s `y = A·x` — is the thinner sibling that *feeds* this motif (it is the per-step kernel the absorbed bodies call) rather than itself absorbing anything; it is the operator/action counterpart of motif 5's operator/data introspection.
5. **Operator introspection** (`assemble-diagonal`) — the L1 form consumes an opaque `LinearOperator[N, N]` and returns operator-**intrinsic data** (`d = diag(A)`) with **no vector operand**: the result is a property of `A` alone. This is the *operator/data* sibling of [`apply_linop`](./apply_linop.md)'s *operator/action* (`y = A·x`) — they share the opaque operator argument and the L0 output-arg mutation idiom but live on opposite sides of the operator/data divide, so `assemble-diagonal` is explicitly **not** an `apply_linop` variant (there is no `x` to be linear in). Laws are operator-introspection identities (diagonal-of-scale, diagonal-of-sum, identity-round-trip), not vector-update laws. One load-bearing non-law: the matrix-free high-order-Nedelec diagonal is *value-approximate* (face-dof sharing in 3D), test-witnessed at the relaxed `rtol = 1.0` — the approximation is structural, not reduction-order noise, and is acceptable because the diagonal feeds an inexact preconditioner. The gate to diagonally-scaled preconditioners (Jacobi / Chebyshev / block-Jacobi / polynomial), all of which reuse the `assemble_diagonal` → `reciprocal` → element-wise-product chain.
6. **Coordinate-space dense direct algebra** (`lu_solve`) — the L1 form operates on a **small dense materialized** matrix in *coordinate space* (`Matrix[k, k]`, `k` = deflation rank / ROM basis size, single to low tens), not the large field-space tensors of dimension `N` that motifs 1–2 act on. `lu_solve(A, b)` solves `A x = b` by a *direct* dense factorization — the small-dense-direct sibling of [`ksp_solve`](./ksp_solve.md) (large-sparse-iterative) on the solve-a-system axis, split by the dense-direct vs sparse-iterative representation/cost distinction (and explicitly **not** an `apply_linop` variant: `lu_solve` *reads* `A`'s entries to factor them, where `apply_linop` applies an opaque action and never sees them). The factorization kernel (full-pivot LU / full-pivot Householder QR / LDLT) is a **load-bearing numerical** variant axis — ROM chooses QR "for maximal stability" on a possibly-ill-conditioned Re/Im-split matrix (`palace/models/romoperator.cpp:762-764` comment, `:765` the `fullPivHouseholderQr.solve`) and rejects a faster LDLT path (`:756-758`); the mathematical value `A⁻¹b` is kernel-independent but the conditioning behaviour is not. The per-step coordinate-solve atom of eigensolver deflation (`QuasiNewtonSolver`, the `k×k` block `S = λI − H`) and ROM online evaluation (`SolvePROM`, the `m×m` PROM matrix); the cycle-022 wave-2 `deflate` / `gram` L2 combinators decompose into `lu_solve` calls.

Shape contracts are declared at boundaries (per the bunsen `contracts::unpack_shape_contract!` style). Single-rank is in scope per `CLAUDE.md`; MPI collectives appear only in lowering themes.

## Working Notes

- The dep-map records **L1-internal** dependencies only. Subsumption chains (`axpy ≺ axpby ≺ axpbypcz`) are stated as algebraic laws in the subsuming operator's entry, not as dep-map edges — both operators stay as siblings in the table.
- Aliasing-aware patterns where aliasing is semantically meaningful (not just buffer reuse) are first-class L1 content; transparent buffer reuse is an L1>L0 lowering concern.
- MPI single-rank scope (per `CLAUDE.md` "Scope") applies uniformly across L1 reductions: the L1 signature never includes a communicator; the L1>L0 lowering reintroduces `MPI_Allreduce` and records bit-deterministic-reduction-order trade-offs.
- Constant-folding fast paths at L0 (e.g., `axpy`'s `α == 1.0` branch, `dot`'s self-dot `&x == &y` branch) are classified as transparent performance tricks and erased at L1 — but only after the critic confirms they are algebraically equivalent to the unfolded form. Load-bearing numerical tricks (the pinned reduction tree) are preserved as explicit non-laws.
- The MINRES / BiCGStab rough-in operators above are emitted by **obstruction** L1>L0 themes — Palace has no L0 realisation (the `KrylovSolver::MINRES` and `KrylovSolver::BICGSTAB` enum cases route to `MFEM_ABORT` at `palace/linalg/ksp.cpp:53-57`). Harvester should not attempt promotion until either (a) Palace gains the implementation or (b) the L0 scope is widened to include vendored MFEM (see open question `bicgstab-mfem-reanchor-policy`).
- `ksp_solve` is the **first firm L1 operator whose primary argument is a structured opaque value** (`Solver[A]`) rather than a raw tensor or scalar. The construction of `Solver[A]` is the [`constructed-operator-factory`](../concepts/constructed-operator-factory.md) concept; the per-method axis collapse is [`variant-absorption`](../concepts/variant-absorption.md); the L0 anchor is [`L0/kspsolver-base-class`](../L0/kspsolver-base-class.md). The variant-axis collapse covers the **implemented** three (`CG`, `GMRES`, `FGMRES`) only; the three aborting enum cases (`MINRES`, `BICGSTAB`, `DEFAULT`) are out-of-scope per CLAUDE.md "Unimplemented Palace stub policy" and remain documented as L1>L0 obstruction themes.
- The L1>L0 mutation-rotation theme for `ksp_solve` is [`ksp-solve-mutation-rotation`](../L1-L0/ksp-solve-mutation-rotation.md) — the first L1>L0 theme whose LHS takes a structured opaque primary argument (`Solver[A]`). It decomposes into the firm sister themes per-step ([`apply-linop-mutation-rotation`](../L1-L0/apply-linop-mutation-rotation.md), [`axpby-mutation-rotation`](../L1-L0/axpby-mutation-rotation.md), [`axpbypcz-mutation-rotation`](../L1-L0/axpbypcz-mutation-rotation.md)) plus four outer-composition absorption rules (timer erase, warning-to-structured-field, counter-to-driver-accumulator, destination-binding).
- `eigsolve` is the **second constructed-operator gate at L1**, composing against `ksp_solve` (the inner linear solver is itself a constructed-operator absorption). It carries the **partial-convergence** semantic (`PartialConverged` status) as a distinguishing feature relative to `ksp_solve`'s soft-fail — the eigenvalue iteration can converge `0 < K < K_max` pairs without being an outright failure, a case `ksp_solve` has no analog for. Per-law anchoring is carried by the §Vocabulary-cohort `eigsolve` bullet, the dep-map row above, and `book/src/L1/eigsolve.md` §Status.
- **FE-assembly + frequency-operator cohort: three NO-L2-ENTRY warrants** — three firm L1 operators decline an L2 entry by anti-mirror warrant (per the 2026-06-01 vocabulary-shift redirect §1d identity-in-named-terms smell — a degenerate mirror is NOT a layer); they are on the planner's STOP-PROPOSING negative list:
  - **`fe_assemble` → NO L2 ENTRY.** Fails the L2-floor warrant on BOTH anti-mirror axes — (1) the fold is a no-carry concatenation homomorphism `Σ_i A(space, term_i)` (order-commutative `AddSubOperator` accumulation, `palace/fem/bilinearform.cpp:77`/`:97`, no sequential carry → no fusion/iteration content, and so no L3 obstruction either), and (2) the per-term leaf `A(space, ·)` is opaque-library-owned (libCEED `CeedOperatorFullAssemble`, no L2 base-primitive composition to unfold). The abstract term-axis fold is ALREADY shared L2 vocabulary ([`linear_combination`](../L2/linear_combination.md) is its `Tensor[N]`-codomain sibling; `fe_assemble` is the `LinearOperator[N,N]`-codomain analog of the same `foldl (+) zero (map leaf terms)` skeleton). No GENUINE-FORM ≥2-pipeline combinator emerges that is not a renamed L1 fold.
  - **`weak_form_term` → NO L2 ENTRY.** An inert `(coefficient, differential-operator)` data leaf that the `fe_assemble` fold quantifies over opaquely at every layer; its per-term realization `A` is the libCEED-owned opaque kernel below the fold. FE vocabulary that lives at L1 as the fold's element type, NOT an L2 composition — no fusion content to shift.
  - **`assemble_frequency_operator` → NO OWN L2/L3 ENTRY.** Already propagated THROUGH [`linear_combination`](../L2/linear_combination.md)'s `operand-category` variant axis as the operator-operand corner (replace-and-propagate, not mine-and-strand). It is not a new fold and owes no own floor — the fold generality lives in `linear_combination`; this operator is its driven-pipeline-only (single-pipeline-by-design) operator-operand specialization. The per-ω rebuild is an affine-operator-family evaluation, not a new L2/L3 form.
