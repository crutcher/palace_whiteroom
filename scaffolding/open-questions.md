### deflate-vs-orthogonalize-over-unification-guard

`deflate` and `orthogonalize` (firm L2) are **RELATED but DISTINCT — do NOT collapse.** Both project a vector against `span(X)`, but differ on three load-bearing axes: (i) **basis precondition** — `orthogonalize` requires `X` orthonormal (`orthog.hpp:22-23`, `book/src/L2/orthogonalize.md:74-76`), `deflate` only full-rank (`nleps.cpp:606-619` stores raw normalized eigenvectors, NOT inter-column-orthogonalized); (ii) **algorithm** — `orthogonalize` does sequential rank-1 subtraction `w ← w − ⟨w,X[j]⟩X[j]` with **no Gram matrix and no solve** (`orthog.hpp:38-89`), `deflate` builds an explicit Gram matrix `XᴴX` (`:526-531`) and **LU-solves** it (`fullPivLu().solve`, `:533-534`); (iii) **projection geometry** — orthogonal `I − XXᴴ` (Gram is `I`, `(XᴴX)⁻¹` drops out) vs oblique/Galerkin `I − X(XᴴX)⁻¹Xᴴ` (the `(XᴴX)⁻¹` is present and **load-bearing**).
